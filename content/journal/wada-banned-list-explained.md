# WADA Banned List Explained: What's Illegal in Sports 2026

The World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) publishes an annual Prohibited List that determines which substances athletes cannot use. Understanding WADA's rules is critical for competitive athletes. [See SARMs legal status](/blog/are-sarms-legal-fda-wada-tga). [Review steroid laws](/blog/steroid-laws-by-country-2026).

## What is WADA?

**WADA** (World Anti-Doping Agency) is the international organization that regulates banned substances in sports.

**WADA Prohibited List** is updated annually (January 1) and includes:
- Anabolic steroids
- Hormones (hGH, EPO)
- Stimulants
- Masking agents
- Diuretics
- Other banned substances

**2026 List:** Published January 1, 2026 (current)

---

## Anabolic Steroids on WADA Banned List

**ALL anabolic steroids are prohibited.** No exceptions.

### Steroids Specifically Listed (2026)
- Testosterone
- Trenbolone
- Nandrolone
- Boldenone
- Stanozolol (Winstrol)
- Oxandrolone (Anavar)
- Oxymetholone (Anadrol)
- Masteron
- Dianabol
- Halotestin
- (And 200+ others)

**Testing:** Athlete testosterone levels are monitored. Testosterone/Epitestosterone ratio above 4:1 is a failed test.

## Other Banned Classes

### Peptide Hormones (All Prohibited)
- Human Growth Hormone (hGH)
- Erythropoietin (EPO)
- IGF-1
- hCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin)

### Beta-2 Agonists
- Clenbuterol (prohibited)
- Salbutamol (allowed for asthma in specific doses)

### Stimulants
- Amphetamines
- Cocaine
- Caffeine (removed from list in 2004 — now allowed)

### Diuretics & Masking Agents
- Furosemide
- Spironolactone
- Probenecid (mask other substances)

## Sport-Specific Rules

### Olympic Sports (Stricter)
- All anabolic steroids prohibited in-competition AND out-of-competition
- Testing year-round
- Longitudinal biological passport tracking changes

### Professional Sports (Variable)
- NFL: Anabolic steroids prohibited, testing 2x/year
- NBA: Anabolic steroids prohibited, testing varies
- MLB: Anabolic steroids prohibited, testing varies
- Boxing: WADA rules + sport-specific rules

### Non-Competitive Sports (No WADA)
- Bodybuilding: No WADA testing (some federations test, most don't)
- Powerlifting: Most federations don't test
- CrossFit: Some competitions test, most don't

## Consequences of Failed Test

### Olympic Athletes
- **First offense:** 4-year ban from sport
- **Second offense:** Lifetime ban
- **Medal forfeiture:** Any medals from positive test removed
- **Public records:** Name published on banned substance database

### Professional Athletes
- **Varies by league**
- NFL: 4-week to full-season suspension (varies by substance)
- NBA: 5-25 game suspension
- MLB: 50-game to full-season suspension

### Amateur Athletes
- **Varies by organization**
- University sports: Immediate ineligibility
- National federations: Sport-specific penalties

## Legitimate Medical Use (Therapeutic Use Exemption)

**Athletes can use banned substances if they have legitimate medical need.**

### Testosterone (TRT)
- Must be diagnosed with hypogonadism (low testosterone)
- Doctor prescribes at therapeutic dose
- Athlete applies for Therapeutic Use Exemption (TUE)
- If approved, athlete can use during competition

### Other Exemptions
- Beta-2 agonists for asthma (Salbutamol allowed)
- Corticosteroids for medical condition (specific rules)
- Some ADHD medications (exemption required)

### How to Apply for TUE
1. Get diagnosis from doctor
2. Get prescription for therapeutic dose
3. Submit TUE application to sport federation
4. Include medical records supporting need
5. Wait for approval (can take weeks)
6. Continue use only if approved

## Testing Methods

### Blood Testing
- Testosterone levels checked
- Growth hormone markers tested
- EPO detected
- Testosterone/Epitestosterone ratio analyzed

### Urine Testing
- Most common method
- Detects metabolites of steroids
- Can detect for weeks/months after use

### Longitudinal Biological Passport
- Tracks athlete's hormone levels over time
- Abnormal changes trigger investigation
- More sophisticated than single tests

## Summary

**Key Facts:**
- ALL anabolic steroids prohibited by WADA
- Testing is year-round for Olympic athletes
- Failed test = 4-year ban (first offense)
- Legitimate medical use requires TUE approval
- Detection windows range from weeks to 18+ months

**For competitive athletes:** Steroids are not worth the risk. One positive test ends amateur career and can affect professional prospects.

**For non-competitive athletes:** No WADA testing in most bodybuilding/powerlifting competitions, so testing is not a concern.
