Growth hormone is not regulated like anabolic steroids, and the difference matters. In one of the three countries below it is a controlled drug, in another it is a prescription medicine with a criminal offence of its own, and in the third it is a prescription medicine but not a controlled substance. This article sets out what the legislation in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada actually says about somatropin, quoted from the legislation itself.

## What the law means by growth hormone

Each statute below names the substance rather than a brand. Somatropin is human growth hormone "produced by recombinant DNA technology", in the words of a licensed product's label.[^2] US law defines "human growth hormone" as "somatrem, somatropin, or an analogue of either of them",[^1] and the UK and Canadian lists below name somatrem alongside somatropin too. ## United States

Growth hormone has a subsection of federal law to itself, in the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act at 21 U.S.C. 333(e).[^1]

- **The offence.** It is an offence to knowingly distribute human growth hormone, or possess it with intent to distribute, "for any use in humans other than the treatment of a disease or other recognized medical condition", where that use has been authorised by the Secretary of Health and Human Services under section 355 of Title 21.[^1]
- **The penalty.** Up to 5 years in prison, a fine, or both. Where the offence involves an individual under 18, the maximum is 10 years.[^1]
- **Enforcement.** For forfeiture purposes a conviction is treated as a felony violation of the Controlled Substances Act, and "the Drug Enforcement Administration is authorized to investigate offenses punishable by this subsection".[^1]

The section is written against distribution and possession with intent to distribute. Licensed somatropin products are prescription medicines: the US label for Genotropin is marked "Rx only".[^2]

## United Kingdom

In the UK, growth hormone is a controlled drug. The Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 lists "Somatotropin", "Somatrem" and "Somatropin" among the Class C drugs in Part III of Schedule 2, in the same run of entries as chorionic gonadotrophin and clenbuterol.[^3]

The Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2001, which apply in England, Wales and Scotland, then relax two things. Part II of their Schedule 4 lists the same three substances.[^4] Regulation 4(3) provides that section 5(1) of the Act, "which prohibits the possession of controlled drugs", does not apply to any drug in Part II of Schedule 4, and regulation 4(2) lifts the import and export offences only where the importation or exportation "is carried out in person for administration to that person".[^5]

In practice that means:

- **Possession** of somatropin is not an offence under section 5(1) of the Act.[^5]
- **Importing** it is outside the import offences only when you carry it in person, for your own use.[^5] A parcel sent to you by post or courier is not importation carried out in person.
- **Supplying** it to anyone else is not covered by either provision.[^5]

The same rules apply to anabolic steroids, and [anabolic steroids and the law in the US, UK, Canada and Australia](/blog/anabolic-steroids-law-us-uk-canada-australia) sets out the history of the in-person rule.

## Canada

Canada treats growth hormone as a prescription drug, not a controlled substance. Health Canada's Prescription Drug List for human use includes "Somatropin" and "Somatrem", both with an effective date of 19 December 2013.[^6]

The Controlled Drugs and Substances Act is where anabolic steroids are controlled: item 23 of Schedule IV is "Anabolic steroids and their derivatives". Somatropin does not appear in Schedule IV.[^7] So the Act's rules for steroids, including its import prohibition, are not the rules that apply to growth hormone; the prescription-drug rules are.

## In sport

Athletes in tested sport answer to a separate set of rules from any of these statutes: the World Anti-Doping Agency's Prohibited List. [WADA's banned list explained](/blog/wada-banned-list-explained) covers what the List includes, how exemptions are granted and what the sanctions are.
