## What nandrolone is

Nandrolone is an anabolic steroid. The product information for the licensed medicine, Deca-Durabolin, describes it as "chemically related to testosterone" and states that, compared with testosterone, "it has an enhanced anabolic and a reduced androgenic activity", as shown in animal bioassays and receptor-binding studies.[^1] It also notes that nandrolone "lacks the C17alpha-alkyl group which is associated with the occurrence of liver dysfunction and cholestasis", the chemical feature found in many oral steroids.[^1]

Nandrolone itself has the molecular formula C18H26O2.[^2] What is sold for injection is almost always nandrolone joined to an acid, called an ester, which changes how slowly it leaves the injection site. Two esters account for every nandrolone listing we stock.

### Decanoate and phenylpropionate: the same hormone, different esters

- **Nandrolone decanoate** is nandrolone with a ten-carbon decanoic acid attached: formula C28H44O3, molecular weight 428.6.[^3] It is the ester in Deca-Durabolin and in most products named "Deca".
- **Nandrolone phenylpropionate (NPP)** carries a 3-phenylpropanoate ester instead: formula C27H34O3, molecular weight 406.6.[^4]

Because the ester adds weight, a label strength is not the same as the amount of nandrolone. With nandrolone at a molecular weight of 274.4,[^2] nandrolone makes up about 64% of the weight of nandrolone decanoate and about 67% of the weight of nandrolone phenylpropionate.[^2][^3][^4]

The ester also changes the timing. The licensed product information says nandrolone decanoate "is slowly released from the injection site into the blood with a half-life of 6 days", and that in the blood the ester is then "rapidly hydrolysed to nandrolone with a half-life of one hour or less".[^1] A study at the University of Sydney compared the two esters in healthy men and found "higher and earlier peaks with the phenylpropionate ester, compared with the decanoate ester". The fall in the men's own testosterone was "most rapid, but briefest" with phenylpropionate. The same study found that the injection site and the volume injected also changed how much nandrolone reached the blood.[^5]

## What the licensed medicine contains: check for peanut oil

The Irish Medicines Board's product information for Deca-Durabolin 100 mg/mL, made by Organon (text revised February 2006), lists exactly what is in each 1 mL glass ampoule: nandrolone decanoate 100 mg, dissolved in "Arachis oil, refined (peanut oil)", with benzyl alcohol.[^1] Because of that oil, the product is contraindicated "in patients allergic to peanuts or soya".[^1] It is stored below 25 °C and not refrigerated.[^1]

This is the most practical point in this guide for anyone comparing products. Injectable nandrolone esters are dissolved in an oil, and the licensed product uses peanut oil.[^1][^5] If a label does not say which oil the solution is made in, the label cannot tell you whether it contains peanut oil.

## Where it is a licensed medicine

In that Irish product information, Deca-Durabolin 100 mg/mL is indicated "for the management of the refractory anaemias associated with such conditions as chronic renal failure (including patients on haemodialysis) aplastic anaemia, anaemia due to cytotoxic therapy associated with malignant disease", and "should only be used under specialist supervision and regular surveillance".[^1] In the United States, the DEA names nandrolone decanoate among the few anabolic steroids prescribed as medicines.[^6]

### What the product information warns about

The same product information lists who must not use it and what to watch for:[^1]

- **Contraindications:** pregnancy, known or suspected prostate or testicular cancer or male breast cancer, and an allergy to peanuts or soya.
- **Warnings:** it "may induce virilisation in female patients" and cause "inhibition of spermatogenesis in males". It is to be used with great caution in people with kidney, heart or liver problems, coronary artery disease, epilepsy or migraine. Glucose tolerance can change, and fluid retention may occur.
- **Undesirable effects listed** include hypertension, raised blood lipids, lower HDL cholesterol, acne, decreased sperm count, increased haemoglobin, benign prostatic hyperplasia and peliosis hepatis (blood-filled cavities in the liver).

These warnings describe the licensed medicine used under supervision. What is known about anabolic steroids used outside medicine is set out in [the health risks of anabolic steroids](/blog/anabolic-steroid-health-risks).

## How to compare listings honestly

### Check which ester the label names

"Deca" and "NPP" are different products, not stronger and weaker versions of one. The label should name the ester, nandrolone decanoate or nandrolone phenylpropionate, not only a brand name such as "Decabold" or "Nandecos". As the chemistry above shows, the same number of milligrams on the label holds a slightly different amount of nandrolone depending on the ester.[^2][^3][^4]

### Price per milligram, not price per vial

Two vials at the same price can hold very different amounts. Multiply the strength by the fill printed on the label (for example 10 mL), then divide the price by the result. Each product page shows the label in its photograph.

### Check the oil, and the batch number

Look for the carrier oil on the label, for the reason given above. A genuine pack also carries a printed batch or lot number and an expiry date, and they match on the box and the vial. For the full checklist, read [how to spot counterfeit steroids and falsified medicines](/blog/how-to-spot-counterfeit-steroids).

### A printed strength is a claim, not a measurement

Labels on this market are often wrong. A 2022 meta-analysis of 19 studies covering 5,413 anabolic steroid samples found 37% were substandard: the right substance in an amount outside the acceptable range. A further 36% were counterfeit: the contents did not match the label.[^7] The authors caution that the studies varied widely.[^7] The cheapest milligram on paper is only the cheapest milligram if the label is accurate.

Every batch we sell is tested by an independent laboratory, and the certificate of analysis for your batch comes with your order. ## The law where you live

Nandrolone is controlled in all four countries below, and in each of them ordering by post is treated differently from simple possession. This is a summary; [anabolic steroids and the law in the US, UK, Canada and Australia](/blog/anabolic-steroids-law-us-uk-canada-australia) quotes the legislation in full.

- **United States.** Schedule III names nandrolone, and covers it "including its salts, esters and ethers", so both decanoate and phenylpropionate.[^8] Possession without a valid prescription is a federal offence; a first offence carries up to one year in prison and a minimum $1,000 fine.[^9] A controlled substance that is a prescription drug may not be "delivered, distributed, or dispensed by means of the Internet without a valid prescription".[^10] Importing a Schedule III substance is unlawful except for "medical, scientific, or other legitimate uses" under federal rules.[^11]
- **United Kingdom.** Nandrolone is named as a Class C drug.[^12] Possession of the steroids in Part II of Schedule 4 is not an offence in England, Wales and Scotland, but since 2012 the import exemption covers only importation "carried out in person for administration to that person".[^13][^14] A parcel sent to you by post or courier is not covered.
- **Canada.** "Anabolic steroids and their derivatives" are in Schedule IV. Simple possession is not an offence, but importing is prohibited "except as authorized under the regulations".[^15]
- **Australia.** Anabolic and androgenic steroidal agents are Schedule 4 (prescription-only), and possession without authority is illegal.[^16] Importing them requires a permission "granted in writing".[^17]

In sport, WADA's 2026 Prohibited List names nandrolone among the anabolic androgenic steroids prohibited "when administered exogenously", at all times, in and out of competition.[^18] Our [guide to the WADA list](/blog/wada-banned-list-explained) explains how it works.
