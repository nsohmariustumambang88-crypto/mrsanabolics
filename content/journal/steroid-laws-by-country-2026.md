# Steroid Laws by Country 2026: Complete Legal Status Guide

Anabolic steroids are controlled substances globally, but penalties vary dramatically by jurisdiction. This guide covers legal status in major countries for 2026. [See related legal article](/blog/anabolic-steroids-law-us-uk-canada-australia). [Learn about WADA prohibited list](/blog/wada-banned-list-explained).

## United States

**Legal Status:** Schedule III Controlled Substance
**Possession (without prescription):** Felony
**Penalties:**
- First offense: Up to 1 year prison, $1,000 fine
- Trafficking: 5-40 years prison depending on quantity
- Import/export: Additional federal charges

**Prosecution trend:** Increasing enforcement. DEA actively investigates UGL operations.

**Legal access:** TRT prescription through licensed doctor (only FDA-approved compounds).

---

## United Kingdom

**Legal Status:** Schedule IV Controlled Substance
**Possession:** Criminal offense (enforcement discretionary)
**Supply:** Illegal, up to 14 years prison
**Import/export:** Serious offense, may face customs seizure + prosecution

**Prosecution trend:** Increasing. Customs routinely seizes international shipments.

**Legal access:** NHS may prescribe for hypogonadism, but TRT access is limited.

---

## Canada

**Legal Status:** Schedule F (Prescription-only)
**Possession (without prescription):** Indictable felony
**Penalties:**
- Possession: Up to 2 years prison
- Trafficking: 2-14 years prison
- Import/export: Additional charges

**Enforcement:** Moderate. Postal service inspects packages.

**Legal access:** Prescription through doctor for hypogonadism (TRT).

---

## Australia

**Legal Status:** Schedule III Controlled Substance
**Possession:** Criminal offense
**Penalties:**
- Possession: Up to 2-5 years prison (varies by state)
- Supply: 5-20 years prison
- Import: Federal offense, up to 5 years

**Enforcement:** Very strict. Border Force actively intercepts shipments.

**Legal access:** Prescription only for TRT (restricted access).

---

## European Union (Varies by Country)

**General Status:** Illegal in all EU countries
**Typical Penalties:**
- Germany: 5 years prison for trafficking, discretionary for possession
- France: 1 year prison for supply, lesser for possession
- Spain: 3 years prison for trafficking
- Netherlands: 6 years prison for trafficking (more lenient on possession)

**Legal access:** Varies; most allow TRT with prescription for documented hypogonadism.

---

## Mexico

**Legal Status:** Controlled substance
**Possession:** Criminal but enforcement is inconsistent
**Penalties:** Vary widely by state

**Access:** Can obtain from pharmacies without prescription (easier than US).

**Enforcement:** Low (corruption/bribery common).

---

## Thailand

**Legal Status:** Controlled substance
**Possession:** Criminal offense
**Penalties:** Up to 10 years prison + fines

**Access:** Available from veterinary suppliers and gyms (open secret).

**Enforcement:** Inconsistent but strict near border.

- Visa applications may be denied
- Background checks reveal offense

### Education

- Graduate school admissions impacted
- Professional certifications may be denied

## Legal Gray Area: TRT (Testosterone Replacement Therapy)

**In every country, TRT is legal with prescription.**

Getting prescribed:
1. Get blood test showing low testosterone (<300 ng/dL)
2. Get doctor diagnosis of hypogonadism
3. Doctor prescribes TRT
4. Pick up at pharmacy with prescription

**Advantages:**
- Legal and safe
- Quality guaranteed
- Medical monitoring provided
- No legal risk

**Disadvantages:**
- Harder to access (requires diagnosis)
- More expensive (no generics in some countries)
- Monitored (can't take "vacation" doses)
