## What testosterone enanthate is

Testosterone enanthate is testosterone with an enanthate ester attached at the 17-beta position.[^1] Enanthate is the older name for heptanoate, a seven-carbon acid; PubChem lists the compound as testosterone heptanoate, molecular formula C26H40O3.[^2] The US prescribing label explains the purpose of the ester in one line: "Esterification of the 17-beta-hydroxy group increases the duration of action of testosterone."[^1] This guide covers the enanthate ester only. We file other testosterone esters separately, and [testosterone cypionate](/shop/injectables/testosterone-cypionate) and [testosterone propionate](/shop/injectables/testosterone-propionate) each have their own page.

In the United States it is an approved prescription medicine. The generic label describes a solution of 200 mg testosterone enanthate per mL in sesame oil, for intramuscular injection, marked "Rx only" and Schedule III.[^1] It is indicated as replacement therapy in males with conditions associated with a deficiency or absence of the body's own testosterone, namely primary hypogonadism and hypogonadotropic hypogonadism. The label adds that its safety and efficacy in men with age-related hypogonadism have not been established.[^1]

## How to compare listings honestly

### Price per milligram, not price per pack

Two packs at the same price can hold very different amounts. Enan 400 from Ultima Pharma is a 10 mL vial at 400 mg per mL: 4,000 mg for $55.00, or about $1.38 per 100 mg. Testomed E 250 from Deus Medical is ten 1 mL ampoules at 250 mg each: 2,500 mg for the same $55.00, or $2.20 per 100 mg. To compare any two listings, multiply the strength by the fill, then divide the price by the result.

### The label should name one compound, one strength, one fill

Check that the label names testosterone enanthate, not a "blend", "mix" or a brand name alone. It should state the strength per mL and the fill (for example "10 mL") or the number and size of ampoules. A vial is multi-dose; ampoules are single-use glass, opened once. That is a difference of packaging, and it is part of what you are paying for. Gomeisa Labs' Testosterone Enanthate 250 mg comes as ten 1 mL ampoules and [Hilma Biocare](/brands/hilma-biocare)'s Testosterone Enanthate 250 as one 10 mL vial: the same 2,500 mg at the labelled strength, packed differently.

### A printed strength is a claim, not a measurement

Everything in the table is what a label states. Labels on this market are often wrong. A 2022 meta-analysis of 19 studies covering 5,413 anabolic steroid samples found 37% were substandard: the right substance in an amount outside the acceptable range. Two thirds of those were under strength.[^3] A further 36% were counterfeit: the contents did not match the label.[^3] The authors caution that the studies varied widely.[^3]

That is why the cheapest milligram on paper is only the cheapest milligram if the label is accurate.

### Batch numbers and certificates

A genuine pack carries a printed batch or lot number and an expiry date, and they match on the box and the vial. Words such as "USP" or "Rx only" are text on a label; they are not evidence that a product is licensed where you live. For the full checklist, read [how to spot counterfeit steroids and falsified medicines](/blog/how-to-spot-counterfeit-steroids).

We do not publish certificates of analysis for these products. If any seller offers you one, [how to read a certificate of analysis](/blog/how-to-read-a-certificate-of-analysis) explains how to check that the laboratory is real, that the test is on its accredited scope, and that the batch number matches your pack.

## The law where you live

Testosterone is regulated in all four countries below, and in each of them ordering by post is treated differently from simple possession. This is a summary; [anabolic steroids and the law in the US, UK, Canada and Australia](/blog/anabolic-steroids-law-us-uk-canada-australia) quotes the legislation in full.

- **United States.** Testosterone is listed in Schedule III.[^4] Possession without a valid prescription is a federal offence; a first offence carries up to one year in prison and a minimum $1,000 fine.[^5] A controlled substance that is a prescription drug may not be "delivered, distributed, or dispensed by means of the Internet without a valid prescription".[^6] Importing a Schedule III substance is unlawful except for "medical, scientific, or other legitimate uses" under federal rules.[^7]
- **United Kingdom.** Testosterone is named as a Class C drug.[^8] Possession of the steroids in Part II of Schedule 4 is not an offence in England, Wales and Scotland, but since 2012 the import exemption covers only importation "carried out in person for administration to that person".[^9][^10] A parcel sent to you by post or courier is not covered.
- **Canada.** Anabolic steroids are in Schedule IV. Simple possession is not an offence, but importing is prohibited "except as authorized under the regulations".[^11]
- **Australia.** Anabolic and androgenic steroidal agents are Schedule 4 (prescription-only), and possession without authority is illegal.[^12] Importing them requires a permission "granted in writing".[^13]
