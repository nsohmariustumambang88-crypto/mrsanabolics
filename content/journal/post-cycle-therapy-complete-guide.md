# Post-Cycle Therapy (PCT) Complete Guide: Recovery Protocols

Post-Cycle Therapy (PCT) is what you do after steroids to restart natural testosterone production. This guide covers complete PCT protocols, timing, medications, and expected recovery timelines.

[See our complete steroid cycle guide for full protocols](/blog/steroid-cycle-guide-beginners).

## Why PCT Is Mandatory

Anabolic steroids suppress natural testosterone production. Without PCT, you remain:
- Testosterone-deficient (low libido, energy, mood)
- Muscularly catabolic (muscle loss accelerates)
- Hormonally imbalanced (estrogen/testosterone ratio off)

PCT restarts testosterone production and preserves the gains you made.

## How Steroids Suppress Testosterone

Steroids work through negative feedback:
1. **Exogenous testosterone/steroids rise** → Signals to your brain: "Stop making testosterone"
2. **Your body shuts down testosterone production** → HPTA (Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Testicular Axis) halts
3. **Natural testosterone drops to near zero** → Your testes stop producing
4. **After steroids end**, your body takes time to restart (weeks to months)

PCT medications jump-start this restart.

## PCT Medications Explained

### Clomid (Clomiphene Citrate) — Most Common

**How it works:** Blocks estrogen feedback, signals brain to restart testosterone production

**Dosing:**
- Standard: 50mg/day for 4 weeks
- Aggressive: 100mg/day for 5 days, then 50mg/day for 3 weeks

**Cost:** $50-100 for full PCT

**Timeline:**
- Day 1: Take first dose
- Day 3-5: Start feeling testosterone-deficient (mood low, libido drops — temporary)
- Week 2: Testosterone production restarts
- Week 3-4: Recover completely
- Post-PCT: Full recovery verified by blood work

**Side effects (temporary):**
- Mood changes (common, temporary)
- Vision changes (rare, temporary)
- Headaches (mild)

### Nolvadex (Tamoxifen) — Alternative to Clomid

**How it works:** Blocks estrogen more directly than Clomid

**Dosing:**
- Standard: 20mg/day for 4 weeks
- More aggressive: 40mg/day for 2 weeks, then 20mg/day for 2 weeks

**Cost:** $50-100 for full PCT

**vs Clomid:** Nolvadex works faster, fewer mood sides, slightly less effective at restarting testosterone

**Most common:** Clomid (more research, better testosterone restart)

### HCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin) — Supportive

**How it works:** Directly stimulates testes to produce testosterone (mimics LH)

**Dosing:**
- Conservative: 250iu 2x weekly for 2-3 weeks
- Aggressive: 500-1000iu daily for 10 days (then stop immediately, too long causes desensitization)

**Cost:** $50-150 depending on source

**Benefit:** Preserves testicular size/function, speeds recovery

**Drawback:** Can raise estrogen (requires monitoring), more expensive than Clomid alone

**When to use:** Optional but helpful, especially after long/harsh cycles

## PCT Protocols by Cycle Type

### After 12-Week Test-Only Cycle

**Standard PCT:**
```
Week 13-16 (start 14 days after last injection):
Clomid: 50mg/day

Or:
Clomid: 100mg/day for 5 days, then 50mg/day for 21 days
```

**Results:** Full testosterone recovery in 4-6 weeks

### After 12-Week Test + Deca Cycle

**Standard PCT:**
```
Week 13-16:
Clomid: 50mg/day
HCG: 250iu 2x weekly (optional but recommended — deca suppresses deeply)
```

**Why HCG?** Nandrolone causes deeper suppression; HCG helps recover faster

**Results:** Full recovery in 6-8 weeks

### After Trenbolone Cycle

**Aggressive PCT (Trenbolone suppresses hard):**
```
Week 9-12 (after 8-week tren cycle):
Clomid: 100mg/day for 5 days, then 50mg/day for 21 days
HCG: 500iu daily for 10 days starting week 1, OR 250iu 2x weekly for 4 weeks
Nolvadex: 20mg/day for 4 weeks (for gyno prevention)
```

**Results:** Full recovery in 6-8 weeks (tren is harsh on HPTA)

### After Long Cycle (16+ weeks)

**Extended PCT:**
```
Week 17-20 (longer suppression = longer recovery):
Clomid: 50-100mg/day
HCG: 250iu 2x weekly for 3-4 weeks
Nolvadex: 10-20mg/day for 4 weeks
```

**Results:** Full recovery in 8-10 weeks

## When to Start PCT

**Timing depends on ester length:**

**Short Esters (Propionate, Acetate):**
- Last injection on Day X
- Start PCT on Day X + 3 days (compounds clear fast)

**Long Esters (Enanthate):**
- Last injection on Day X  
- Start PCT on Day X + 14 days (1.5-2 weeks after last shot)

**Very Long Esters (Undecanoate):**
- Last injection on Day X
- Start PCT on Day X + 21 days (3 weeks after last shot)

**Rule:** Wait until steroid has largely cleared before starting PCT (HCG works best when exogenous hormones are low).

## PCT Blood Work

**Before PCT:**
- Testosterone (free and total — should be suppressed)
- Estradiol
- Prolactin
- Liver/kidney function

**2 weeks into PCT:**
- Testosterone (should be rising)
- Estradiol (should normalize as testosterone rises)

**1 month after PCT ends:**
- Testosterone (should return to baseline)
- Estradiol
- Full panel (liver, kidney, lipids)

**Verification:** Testosterone in normal range (300-1000 ng/dL) = PCT successful

## PCT Timeline & What to Expect

### Week 1 of PCT
- Feel depleted (testosterone is recovering, still low)
- Mood may be low (temporary)
- Libido may be low (temporary)
- Energy is low (temporary)
- Muscle retention good (clomid stimulates testosterone)

### Week 2-3 of PCT
- Energy increases
- Mood improves
- Libido returns
- Strength starts coming back
- Muscle retention excellent

### Week 4-5 Post-PCT
- Full recovery (energy, mood, libido)
- Strength fully recovered
- Blood work should show testosterone in normal range

### Weeks 5-8 Post-PCT
- Extended recovery period
- Verify with blood work (testosterone baseline)

## Common PCT Mistakes

**Mistake 1: No PCT at all**
- Result: Months of low testosterone, muscle loss, depression
- Risk: Real (seen frequently)

**Mistake 2: Starting PCT too early**
- Result: PCT medications fight exogenous steroids still in system, less effective
- Solution: Wait 2-3 weeks (depending on ester) before starting

**Mistake 3: Using wrong dosages**
- Result: Incomplete recovery, prolonged low testosterone
- Solution: Use 50mg+ clomid (or equivalent nolvadex), not lower

**Mistake 4: No blood work verification**
- Result: Don't know if recovered or still suppressed
- Solution: Blood test 2 weeks post-PCT (cost ~$100-200)

**Mistake 5: Starting new cycle before full recovery**
- Result: HPTA remains suppressed long-term, chronic low testosterone
- Solution: Wait 4-8 weeks minimum between PCT and new cycle

## PCT for Female Athletes

**Females:** No PCT needed (female testosterone production is minimal; naturally low)

**Recovery:** Takes 4-8 weeks for normal hormone levels to resume on their own

**Monitoring:** Blood work to verify estradiol/testosterone normalized

## Advanced PCT (After Harsh Cycles)

For guys running very suppressive cycles (long tren, 20+ week cycles), optional additions:

**Cabergoline (0.25mg 2x weekly):**
- Lowers prolactin (tren elevates it)
- Supports libido recovery

**Aromasin/Arimidex (optional):**
- Mild aromatase inhibitor
- Some recommend during first 2 weeks of PCT
- Controversial (may delay recovery)

**AI Generally NOT recommended during PCT** — your body needs estrogen to recover; blocking it delays testosterone restart.

## PCT Doesn't Guarantee Full Recovery

Important: Even with perfect PCT, some users experience:
- Slower testosterone recovery (genetics)
- Temporary ED (erectile dysfunction) — resolves in 2-8 weeks
- Mood issues — resolves within 2 weeks of PCT starting
- Small amount of muscle loss (1-3 lbs) — unavoidable

PCT minimizes these, doesn't eliminate them.

## Cruising vs PCT (Alternative Approach)

Some experienced users stay on low-dose testosterone year-round instead of PCT:

**Cruising (TRT dose maintenance):**
- 150-250mg testosterone/week (long-term)
- No PCT needed (continuous)
- Keeps testosterone normal between blasts
- Requires commitment to lifelong TRT

**Traditional PCT approach:**
- Higher dose cycles
- Full PCT between
- Off-periods (0 gear)

**Most users:** PCT approach (allows natural recovery)

[PCT is mandatory after every steroid cycle](/blog/steroid-cycle-guide-beginners). Standard protocol is Clomid 50mg/day for 4 weeks, started 14-21 days after last injection.

Verify recovery with blood work 2 weeks post-PCT. Full recovery typically takes 4-8 weeks depending on cycle severity.

Don't skip PCT.
