## Why Cut on Steroids?

Cutting (calorie deficit) naturally suppresses testosterone and muscle loss is inevitable. Steroids preserve muscle mass during cuts by:
1. Maintaining elevated protein synthesis despite deficit
2. Increasing metabolic rate (thermogenesis)
3. Protecting muscle from catabolism
4. Improving workout intensity despite lower calories

Result: Lose fat while preserving (or even gaining) muscle.

## Calorie Guidelines for Cutting

**General rule:** -500 cals from maintenance = 1 lb fat loss per week (sustainable)

Example: 200 lb person at maintenance 2,800 cals
- Cutting calories: 2,300 cals/day
- Protein: 200g (0.8g per lb bodyweight, higher during cut)
- Carbs: 250-300g
- Fats: 65-75g

## General Cutting Principles

**Apply to all cutting stacks:**

1. **Protein is high (0.8-1g per lb)**  — Prevents muscle loss in deficit
2. **Carbs are moderate (2-3g per lb)** — Supports workout intensity
3. **Fats are lower (0.3-0.4g per lb)** — Hormonal needs met but controlled
4. **Cardio is essential** — 30-45 min, 4-6x weekly (supplements steroids)
5. **Lifting intensity maintained** — Don't reduce weight/volume on cut
6. **Deficit should be -500 to -750 cals** — Anything more risks muscle loss
7. **Duration: 8-14 weeks** — Longer cuts risk hormonal issues

## Pre-Cutting Blood Work

Test testosterone, estradiol, prolactin, liver/kidney function, lipids, hemoglobin/hematocrit.

**During cut (week 6-8):**
Recheck lipids (change dramatically on cutting compounds).
