## The Three Supply Channels

### 1. Pharmaceutical (Prescription Only)
**Quality:** Guaranteed ✅
**Cost:** $100-300/month
**Verification:** Easy (official manufacturer)
**Legal:** Requires prescription
**Availability:** Pharmacies only

**How to access:**
- Get diagnosed with hypogonadism (low testosterone)
- Doctor prescribes TRT (Testosterone Replacement Therapy)
- Pick up at pharmacy with prescription

**Brands:**
- Testosterone enanthate (generic, $100-200/month)
- Testosterone cypionate (generic, $100-200/month)
- Androgel (expensive, $400+/month)

**Verification:** Pharmacy receipt + prescription = authentic

### 2. Underground Laboratories (UGL) — Legal Gray Area
**Quality:** Highly variable (authentication required)
**Cost:** $50-150/month (cheaper but riskier)
**Verification:** Difficult (requires lab testing)
**Legal:** Illegal without prescription

**How UGLs work:**
- Small operation manufactures steroids in unregulated facility
- May or may not follow good manufacturing practices
- Quality varies dramatically (some as good as pharma, some contaminated)
- Price attractive because no regulation/licensing costs

**Verification options:**
1. **Certificate of Analysis (CoA)** — Lab tested the batch. Verify the lab independently.
2. **Community reviews** — Search forums for user reviews of that specific batch
3. **Price comparison** — Realistic cost for testosterone enanthate is $50-100/month
4. **Business registration** — Verify the company actually exists legally

**Red flags:**
- No CoA available
- New website (under 1 year old)
- Prices unrealistically low ($20/month = fake)
- No way to contact supplier
- No customer reviews

---

### 3. Black Market / Street Level
**Quality:** Unknown ❌
**Cost:** Variable
**Verification:** Impossible
**Risk:** Highest (scams common)

**What to expect:**
- 40-60% of street testosterone is counterfeit
- May contain wrong substance entirely
- May contain dangerous contaminants
- No recourse if product is fake

**Why avoid:** Not worth the risk. Cost savings don't justify the danger.

## How to Verify Testosterone Source

### Step 1: Verify Business Registration
1. Look up company name in business registries
2. Check domain age (WHOIS)
3. Find physical address
4. Look for years in business (minimum 3-5 years credibility)

### Step 2: Verify Certificate of Analysis
1. Get the lab name from the CoA
2. Find the lab independently via Google (don't use supplier link)
3. Verify the lab is accredited (ILAC, A2LA)
4. Call the lab: "Did you test batch [number]?"
5. Lab confirms or denies — they have records

### Step 3: Check Community Reviews
1. Search forums (Reddit, bodybuilding forums)
2. Look for reviews from past 6 months (old reviews irrelevant)
3. Check for both positive AND negative reviews (all positive = fake reviews)
4. Look for specific results (what they experienced)

### Step 4: Price Sanity Check
- Pharmaceutical testosterone: $100-300/month
- UGL testosterone: $50-150/month
- Anything under $30/month: Likely counterfeit
