## What trenbolone is

Trenbolone is an anabolic steroid. US federal regulations list it by name among the anabolic steroids in Schedule III,[^1] and the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) lists it among the anabolic androgenic steroids.[^2] Both identify it by its chemical name, 17β-hydroxyestr-4,9,11-trien-3-one.[^1][^2] Its molecular formula is C18H22O2.[^3]

In the United States, the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) names trenbolone as one of three steroids, with boldenone and mibolerone, that "are used only in veterinary medicine".[^4] It is also one of the steroids the DEA's forensic laboratory database meets most often. That database collects the results of drug analyses by federal, state and local forensic laboratories, and it names trenbolone among the five anabolic steroids most frequently encountered: 160 reports in 2024, and 7,413 over all time.[^4]

### Acetate, enanthate and hexahydrobenzylcarbonate: one steroid, three esters

The packs in our listings name trenbolone joined to an acid, called an ester, which is how it is sold for injection. Three esters appear on them:

- **Trenbolone acetate:** formula C20H24O3, molecular weight 312.4.[^5] It is the form in the cattle implants described below.[^6]
- **Trenbolone enanthate** (the heptanoate ester): formula C25H34O3, molecular weight 382.5.[^7]
- **Trenbolone hexahydrobenzylcarbonate**, also called trenbolone cyclohexylmethylcarbonate, and known by the brand name Parabolan: formula C26H34O4, molecular weight 410.5.[^8]

Because the ester adds weight, a label strength is not the same as the amount of trenbolone. With trenbolone at a molecular weight of 270.4,[^3] it makes up about 87% of the weight of trenbolone acetate, about 71% of the enanthate and about 66% of the hexahydrobenzylcarbonate.[^3][^5][^7][^8] A mix labelled 50 mg of each of the three esters therefore holds about 112 mg of trenbolone in its 150 mg.[^3][^5][^7][^8]

## What regulators have approved it for: cattle

In the United States, the FDA approvals for trenbolone are set out in the Code of Federal Regulations, and all of them are for trenbolone acetate in pellet implants for growing beef cattle: on its own,[^6] with estradiol,[^9] or with estradiol benzoate.[^10] Each implant goes under the skin of the ear only, and the regulations rule out dairy animals and animals intended for breeding.[^6][^9][^10]

Australia's Poisons Standard puts trenbolone in Schedule 4, the schedule for prescription-only medicines and prescription animal remedies, except for implant preparations "for growth promotion in animals", which are in Schedule 5.[^11]

The European Union went the other way. Council Directive 96/22/EC requires member states to prohibit (provisionally, in the directive's word) giving farm animals substances with androgenic action "by any means whatsoever".[^12] Trenbolone is one: WADA lists it among the anabolic androgenic steroids.[^2] The directive allows narrow exceptions for treatment by a veterinarian of clearly identified animals, and it requires member states to prohibit imports of farm animals given such substances outside those rules, and of meat from them.[^12]

In the sources above, the only approved trenbolone products are the FDA's cattle implants. We found no licensed human product information for trenbolone to quote, in contrast to the licensed Deca-Durabolin label in our nandrolone guide. None of the listings below is one of those implants: every one is a solution for injection, as its listing states.

## How to compare listings honestly

### Check which ester, or which mix, the label names

Acetate, enanthate and hexahydrobenzylcarbonate are different products, not stronger and weaker versions of one. The label should name the ester, not only a brand name such as "Hexos" or "Tremilad". As the chemistry above shows, the same number of milligrams on the label holds a different amount of trenbolone depending on the ester.[^3][^5][^7][^8]

### Price per milligram, not price per pack

Two packs at the same price can hold very different amounts. Multiply the strength by the fill printed on the label, then divide the price by the result. A box of ten 1 mL ampoules holds the same volume as one 10 mL vial; a 5 mL vial holds half as much. Each product page shows the label in its photograph.

### Read the active ingredient, not the name

"MTren" and "Tren" differ by one letter, and the products differ by more than that. Read the active ingredient printed on the pack before you compare anything else.

### A prescription symbol does not make it a medicine

Some packs in our photographs carry a prescription symbol (℞) or the words "as directed by the physician". They are part of the printed pack, and they do not mean a regulator has approved the product. In the United States the DEA says trenbolone is used only in veterinary medicine,[^4] and the FDA approvals for it are cattle implants.[^6][^9][^10]

### Check the batch number

A genuine pack carries a printed batch or lot number and an expiry date, and they match on the box and the vial or ampoule. For the full checklist, read [how to spot counterfeit steroids and falsified medicines](/blog/how-to-spot-counterfeit-steroids).

### A printed strength is a claim, not a measurement

Labels on this market are often wrong. A 2022 meta-analysis of 19 studies covering 5,413 anabolic steroid samples estimated that 36% were counterfeit and a further 37% were substandard. The authors found products with no active ingredient, the wrong active ingredient, or an amount different from the label.[^14] They also caution that the studies varied widely.[^14] The cheapest milligram on paper is only the cheapest milligram if the label is accurate.

Every batch we sell is tested by an independent laboratory, and the certificate of analysis for your batch comes with your order. Check ours the same way you would check anyone's: [how to read a certificate of analysis](/blog/how-to-read-a-certificate-of-analysis) explains how to confirm the laboratory is real, that the test is on its accredited scope, and that the batch number matches your pack. [What lab tests can and cannot prove](/blog/hplc-mass-spectrometry-lab-testing-explained) explains which tests can show what is in a vial, and which can show how much of it there is.

## The law where you live {fold}

Trenbolone is controlled in all four countries below. This is a summary; [anabolic steroids and the law in the US, UK, Canada and Australia](/blog/anabolic-steroids-law-us-uk-canada-australia) quotes the legislation in full.

- **United States.** Schedule III names trenbolone, and covers it "including its salts, esters and ethers", so the acetate, enanthate and hexahydrobenzylcarbonate alike. Methyltrienolone is listed separately in the same schedule.[^1] Possessing a controlled substance is a federal offence unless it was obtained from a practitioner, directly or on a valid prescription or order; a first offence carries up to one year in prison and a minimum $1,000 fine.[^15]
- **United Kingdom.** Trenbolone is named as a Class C drug, and the Class C entry also covers its esters.[^13]
- **Canada.** Schedule IV lists "Anabolic steroids and their derivatives" and names trenbolone among them.[^16]
- **Australia.** Trenbolone is a Schedule 4 substance, and Appendix D of the Poisons Standard lists anabolic steroidal agents among the poisons "for which possession without authority is illegal".[^11]

In sport, WADA's 2026 Prohibited List names trenbolone and metribolone among the anabolic androgenic steroids prohibited at all times, in and out of competition.[^2] Our [guide to the WADA list](/blog/wada-banned-list-explained) explains how it works.
