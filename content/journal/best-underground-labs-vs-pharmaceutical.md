## Pharmaceutical Steroids (Prescription)

**Source:** Licensed pharmaceutical manufacturer (Pfizer, Watson, Actavis)
**Quality:** FDA/EMA regulated
**Cost:** $150-300/month
**Verification:** Easy (pharmacy receipt)
**Risk:** Zero (guaranteed quality)

### What They Are
- Made in FDA-inspected facilities
- Sterile under clean-room conditions
- Exact concentration verified
- Same batches produced consistently
- Shelf-stable if stored properly

### Pharmaceutical Brands
- **Testosterone enanthate** (Watson, Actavis, generic)
- **Testosterone cypionate** (Pfizer, Watson, generic)
- **Nandrolone decanoate** (Deca-Durabolin, generic)
- **Oxandrolone** (Anavar — very expensive)

### How to Access
1. Get diagnosed with hypogonadism (low testosterone)
2. Get prescription from doctor
3. Pick up at pharmacy with insurance or pay out-of-pocket
4. Same product every time

### Advantages
- 100% guaranteed authentic
- Verified concentration (not under-dosed)
- Sterile and clean (no infections)
- Medical monitoring available
- No legal risk (with prescription)

### Disadvantages
- Expensive ($150-300/month)
- Requires doctor diagnosis
- Fewer compound options (only FDA-approved)
- Monitored dosing (doctor controls amount)
- Limited to legitimate compounds

## Underground Lab (UGL) Steroids

**Source:** Illegal manufacturer
**Quality:** Highly variable (50-100% of label claim)
**Cost:** $50-150/month (cheaper)
**Verification:** Requires testing
**Risk:** Moderate-high (counterfeits exist)

### What They Are
- Made in non-regulated facilities
- Variable sterility (depends on manufacturer)
- Concentration may vary from label
- Quality varies batch-to-batch
- May contain contaminants

### Common UGL Brands
- Geneza Pharmaceuticals
- Balkan Pharmaceuticals
- Dragon Pharma
- British Dragon
- Rohm Labs
- (Many others, variable quality)

### How to Access
1. Find through community (forum recommendation)
2. Verify CoA (Certificate of Analysis)
3. Order through encrypted channel
4. Pay in cryptocurrency
5. Receive discreet package
6. Inject (hope quality is real)

### Advantages
- Much cheaper ($50-150/month vs $150-300)
- More compound options (tren, masteron, etc.)
- More control over dosing (order specific amounts)
- No prescription needed
- Faster access (no doctor approval needed)

### Disadvantages
- Illegal (prosecution risk)
- Quality variable (may be under-dosed or fake)
- Counterfeit risk (60-80% market is fake)
- Contaminants possible (infections, abscesses)
- No medical monitoring
- Scam risk (supplier disappears after payment)

## Red Flags When Choosing UGL

- Only accepts wire transfer
- Can't contact supplier

### Lower-Risk UGL (Still risky)

- CoA available
- Multiple customer reviews
- Realistic pricing
- Multiple payment options

## How to Reduce UGL Risk

1. **Verify CoA independently**
   - Call lab, confirm batch tested
   - Check lab is accredited
   - Verify batch number matches your vial

2. **Read community reviews**
   - Search Reddit, forums
   - Look for past 6 months
   - Check both positive and negative
   - Look for people reporting blood work

3. **Test before using**
   - Get blood work at week 4 (see testosterone level)
   - Lab test sample (cost $100-200)
   - Visual inspection (look for red flags)

4. **Small test order first**
   - Order 1-2 vials from new supplier
   - Test quality before full order
   - Reduces risk of larger loss
