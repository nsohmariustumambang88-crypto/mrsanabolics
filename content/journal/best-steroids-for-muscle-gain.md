## What Separates Muscle-Building Steroids from Others?

Muscle growth requires three things:
1. **High protein synthesis** — the rate your body builds muscle proteins
2. **IGF-1 and growth factors** — signals that trigger hypertrophy
3. **Androgen receptor density** — how many "growth signals" muscle cells can receive

The best muscle-building steroids maximize all three.

## The Muscle-Building Priority Hierarchy

**1. Training (60% of results)**
- Progressive overload (increase weight weekly)
- 4-5 days/week in gym
- Hit each muscle 2x/week
- Compound movements (bench, squat, deadlift)

**2. Nutrition (25% of results)**
- 1g protein per lb bodyweight
- 500+ calorie surplus daily
- Whole foods (rice, chicken, beef, eggs)
- Consistent meal timing

**3. Steroids (15% of results)**
- Even the best steroid won't build muscle without training
- A beginner on trenbolone with bad training gains less than an intermediate on testosterone with perfect training

**The math:** A beginner naturally might gain 1-2 lbs muscle/month. On steroids, they gain 4-6 lbs/month. But if they don't train hard, steroids barely double the rate.

## The Role of Compound vs. Isolation

**Compounds build the most muscle:**
- Bench press (chest, shoulders, triceps)
- Squat (quads, glutes, hamstrings, core)
- Deadlift (back, glutes, hamstrings)
- Rows (back, shoulders)
- OHP (shoulders, triceps, chest)

**Isolation exercises refine:**
- Bicep curls, lateral raises, leg curls, etc.

**Steroid cycle split example:**
- Day 1: Chest/Shoulders (bench, OHP, rows)
- Day 2: Legs (squat, deadlift, leg press)
- Day 3: Rest
- Day 4: Back/Shoulders (rows, pull-ups, OHP)
- Day 5: Arms/Accessories (isolation work)
- Days 6-7: Rest

**Why this works:** Hits each major muscle 2x/week, allows compound movements when strength is highest (CNS fresh), uses isolation as finisher.

## The Muscle vs. Fat Gain Tradeoff

All steroid-accelerated muscle gain comes with some fat gain. The ratio depends on:
- **Calorie surplus (bigger = more fat)**
- **Steroid choice (test/nand = more water; tren = dry)**
- **Training quality (poor training = more fat)**
- **Genetics (some gain muscle:fat in 5:1 ratio, others 2:1)**

**Best approach:** Aim for 1 lb fat gain per 5 lbs muscle gain. This means:
- 30 lb cycle gain = 25 lbs muscle + 5 lbs fat
- Diet and training quality determine if you hit this or go 50/50
