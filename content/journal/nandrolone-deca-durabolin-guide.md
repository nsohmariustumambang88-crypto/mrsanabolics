# Nandrolone (Deca-Durabolin) Guide: Dosage, Effects, and Stack

Nandrolone is one of the most popular steroids for muscle gain and joint health. This guide covers dosing, expected results, side effects, and why nandrolone is considered one of the best compounds for bulking.

## What Is Nandrolone?

Nandrolone is a synthetic steroid derived from testosterone with a modified structure that increases anabolic effects (2.5x more anabolic than testosterone) while decreasing androgenic effects (0.4x less androgenic than testosterone).

**Two forms exist:**
- **Nandrolone Decanoate (Deca-Durabolin):** 10-14 day duration, most popular
- **Nandrolone Phenylpropionate (NPP):** 3-4 day duration, less common, faster-acting

Most users choose Deca for its stable hormone levels and less frequent dosing.

## Why Nandrolone Is Popular

**Advantages:**
1. **Joint healing** — Increases collagen synthesis, reduces joint pain
2. **High anabolic rating** — 2.5x more anabolic than testosterone
3. **Low androgenic effects** — Less acne, hair loss, prostate issues than testosterone
4. **Excellent synergy** — Stacks perfectly with any other steroid
5. **Affordable** — $100-200/month from UGL sources
6. **Proven results** — Decades of data on safety and effectiveness

**Disadvantages:**
1. **Slow-acting** — Takes 5-6 weeks to feel full effects
2. **Long detection window** — Detectable in drug tests for 18 months
3. **Deca dick** — Erectile dysfunction (common, paradoxical)
4. **Water retention** — Causes bloating (manageable)
5. **Progestin effects** — Can cause gynecomastia (rare but possible)
6. **Cannot run alone** — Must stack with testosterone

## Nandrolone Dosage

Nandrolone is dosed in **mg per week** and must ALWAYS be stacked with testosterone.

### Beginner Cycle

**Dosage:**
- Nandrolone Decanoate: 300 mg/week (150mg Monday + 150mg Thursday)
- Testosterone Enanthate: 300 mg/week (150mg Monday + 150mg Thursday)
- Duration: 12 weeks

**Results:** 20-30 lbs muscle gain, improved joint health

**Why this works:** Nandrolone is slow-acting, so higher doses are needed for results within 12 weeks. Starting at 300mg lets you see results by week 6-8.

## Nandrolone Side Effects

### Why Nandrolone Causes Side Effects Differently

Nandrolone doesn't aromatize to estrogen (unlike testosterone), but it binds to **progesterone receptors**, causing unique side effects:

### "Deca Dick" (Erectile Dysfunction)

This is the most common and most frustrating side effect.

**Why it happens:**
- Nandrolone suppresses dopamine production
- Dopamine is needed for erections
- Low dopamine = low libido + erectile dysfunction

**Management:**
- **Cabergoline:** 0.25-0.5mg twice weekly (dopamine agonist)
- **Or Bromocriptine:** 2.5mg daily (cheaper alternative)
- **Or HCG:** 250iu twice weekly (stimulates natural testosterone)

Most users find that adding cabergoline completely solves the problem.

### Progestin Gynecomastia

Unlike testosterone-induced gyno (from estrogen), nandrolone can cause gyno through progesterone receptors.

**Management:**
- Nolvadex 10-20mg daily (blocks progesterone effects)
- Avoid high-dose nandrolone (300-400mg is "safe zone")
- Monitor nipple sensitivity weekly

### Water Retention

Nandrolone causes mild to moderate water retention.

**Management:**
- Keep sodium under 2,500mg/day
- Increase potassium intake
- Mild diuretic if extreme (not recommended)
- Resolves 2-3 weeks post-cycle

- Acne (mild, since nandrolone is low-androgenic)
- Hair loss (minimal risk)
- High blood pressure (mild, manageable)
- Liver stress (minimal, nandrolone is not liver-toxic)

## Why Nandrolone Cannot Be Run Alone

**Critical:** Nandrolone MUST be combined with testosterone. Running nandrolone alone causes:

1. **Complete testosterone suppression** — Your natural test drops to zero
2. **No testosterone activity** — Nandrolone doesn't fully replace testosterone's functions
3. **Severe sexual dysfunction** — More pronounced without baseline testosterone
4. **Poor recovery** — Testosterone is needed for recovery
5. **Muscle loss post-cycle** — Without testosterone, gains are lost

**Always stack with testosterone at minimum.**

## Nandrolone Myths (Debunked)

**Myth:** "Nandrolone permanently lowers testosterone"
- **Truth:** Temporary suppression only. Testosterone returns 4-8 weeks post-cycle.

**Myth:** "Nandrolone is safer because it's low-androgenic"
- **Truth:** Safety is compound-dependent, not androgenicity-dependent. Nandrolone carries its own risks (deca dick, progestin effects).

**Myth:** "You need an AI (aromatase inhibitor) on nandrolone"
- **Truth:** Nandrolone doesn't aromatize. You need Nolvadex instead (for progesterone effects).

**Myth:** "Deca dick goes away once you stop"
- **Truth:** Deca dick resolves 2-4 weeks after stopping (dopamine normalizes). If it persists, use cabergoline.

## Nandrolone Detection in Drug Tests

**Important for competitive athletes:** Nandrolone is detectable for 18 months post-injection through metabolite testing.

**Specifics:**
- Nandrolone metabolites: 19-norandrosterone and 19-noretiocholanolone
- Detection window: Up to 18 months
- WADA: Prohibited in all sports at all times
