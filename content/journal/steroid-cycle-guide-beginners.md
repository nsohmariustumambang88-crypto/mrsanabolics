# Complete Steroid Cycle Guide for Beginners: 12-Week Blueprint

This is a complete, step-by-step guide for someone running their first steroid cycle. Follow this to maximize gains while minimizing mistakes and side effects. Start with [our beginner steroids guide](/blog/best-steroids-for-beginners) first, and review [side effects](/blog/steroid-side-effects-complete-guide) before starting.

## Pre-Cycle Requirements (4 Weeks Before)

### 1. Get Baseline Blood Work
**What to test:**
- Testosterone (free and total)
- Estradiol
- Prolactin
- Liver function (AST, ALT, GGT)
- Lipid panel (cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides)
- Hemoglobin/hematocrit (red blood cells)
- Kidney function (creatinine, BUN)

**Cost:** $150-300
**Why:** Establishes baseline before cycle; used to compare post-cycle

### 2. Establish Training Program
- 4-5 days/week in gym
- Focus on compound movements (bench, squat, deadlift, rows)
- Progressive overload (increase weight weekly)
- Track all workouts (log every set/rep)

**You won't gain muscle without hard training, steroids won't change this.**

### 3. Dial In Nutrition
**Calories:** +500/day surplus
**Protein:** 1g per lb bodyweight
**Carbs:** 2-3g per lb bodyweight
**Fats:** 0.4-0.5g per lb bodyweight

**Example (200 lb person):**
- Protein: 200g (800 cals)
- Carbs: 400-600g (1600-2400 cals)
- Fats: 80-100g (720-900 cals)
- Total: 3120-4260 calories/day

### 4. Plan Your Cycle
**First cycle recommendation:**
- Testosterone Enanthate 400mg/week
- Duration: 12 weeks
- Cost: $600-800 total

### 5. Order Supplies
- Syringes (25G 1.5" needles, 50-100 pack): $20-30
- Alcohol pads: $5-10
- Band-aids: $3
- Sharps container: $10-15
- PCT (Clomid or Nolvadex): $80-150

**Total supplies cost:** $120-200

## Week 13-15: Post-Cycle Therapy (PCT)

**Why PCT matters:** Without it, testosterone stays suppressed for 8-12 weeks. PCT jumpstarts recovery.

### PCT Protocol (Choose One)

**Option 1: Nolvadex (Tamoxifen)**
```
Week 13-15: 20mg/day
Week 16-17: 10mg/day
Total cost: $80-120
```

**Option 2: Clomid (Clomiphene)**
```
Week 13-15: 50mg/day
Week 16-17: 25mg/day
Total cost: $80-150
```

**Why PCT works:**
- Blocks estrogen in pituitary
- Signals body to resume testosterone production
- Restores LH/FSH
- Jumpstarts testicular function

## Weeks 16+: Recovery Phase

### Blood Work (Week 16)
**Test:**
- Testosterone (should be recovering)
- Estradiol (should be normalizing)
- Liver function (should be normalizing)
- Lipids (should be normalizing)

**Timeline of recovery:**
- Week 16: Testosterone 400-600 ng/dL (recovering)
- Week 20: Testosterone 600-800 ng/dL (mostly recovered)
- Week 24: Testosterone 700-1000 ng/dL (fully recovered for most)

### What to Do During Recovery
- Maintain training (don't deload)
- Eat in slight deficit if fat gain was high
- Sleep 8+ hours
- Avoid alcohol (helps recovery)
- Supplement (multivitamin, omega-3)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Starting Too High a Dose
- **Wrong:** 600mg/week on first cycle
- **Right:** 400mg/week (proven, manageable)
- **Why:** More hormone ≠ more gains linearly after a point

### ❌ Mistake 2: Skipping Training
- **Wrong:** Expecting steroids to build muscle without training
- **Right:** Training hard, then steroids amplify results
- **Why:** Steroids require stimulus to work

### ❌ Mistake 3: Skipping Bloodwork
- **Wrong:** Never testing testosterone levels
- **Right:** Testing week 6 to confirm real steroid
- **Why:** Proves steroid is real, catches side effects early

### ❌ Mistake 4: No PCT
- **Wrong:** Stopping cold turkey after 12 weeks
- **Right:** Running PCT weeks 13-17
- **Why:** PCT cuts recovery time from 12 weeks to 6 weeks

### ❌ Mistake 5: Wrong Expectations
- **Wrong:** Expecting 50 lbs muscle in 12 weeks
- **Right:** Expecting 15-25 lbs muscle
- **Why:** Realistic expectations = sustainable goals

## Signs You Need to Stop Immediately

- **Extreme mood swings** (wanting to hurt people)
- **Chest pain** (possible cardiac event)
- **Jaundice/dark urine** (liver failure)
- **Extreme gynecomastia** (uncontrollable)
- **Allergic reaction** (difficulty breathing, swelling)

**If any occur: Stop, see doctor, tell truth about steroid use**

## After First Cycle: What's Next?

**Retention phase (6-12 weeks):**
- Train normally
- Maintain diet
- Don't run another cycle immediately
- Let body fully recover

**Next cycle (if pursuing):**
- Run again 12+ weeks later (minimum 6-week break)
- Same dose worked = keep it
- If sides were bad = lower dose next time
- If minimal sides = can add another compound
