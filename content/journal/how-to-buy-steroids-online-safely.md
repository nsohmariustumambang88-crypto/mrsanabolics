## Is it legal to buy steroids online?

In most countries, buying without a prescription and having a parcel posted to you are treated differently, and the parcel is often the bigger problem. In brief:

- **United States.** Anabolic steroids are listed in Schedule III.[^1] A controlled substance that is a prescription drug may not be "delivered, distributed, or dispensed by means of the Internet without a valid prescription".[^2] Importing a Schedule III substance is unlawful unless it is imported "for medical, scientific, or other legitimate uses" under federal rules.[^3]
- **United Kingdom.** The import exemption for the steroids listed in Part II of Schedule 4 covers only importation "carried out in person for administration to that person".[^4] A parcel sent to you by post or courier is not carried in person.
- **Canada.** Anabolic steroids are in Schedule IV, and "Except as authorized under the regulations, no person shall import into Canada" a substance in that schedule.[^5]
- **Australia.** "Anabolic or androgenic substances" are listed in Schedule 7A, and importing them is prohibited unless the importer holds a permission "granted in writing".[^6]

Our article on [anabolic steroids and the law in the US, UK, Canada and Australia](/blog/anabolic-steroids-law-us-uk-canada-australia) quotes the legislation for each country in full, including possession and supply.

## Why buying steroids online is risky

### Counterfeit and mislabelled products

The best summary of what is inside black-market steroids is a 2022 systematic review and meta-analysis. It pooled 19 studies from Europe and Brazil, covering 5,413 samples.[^7]

- **36%** were counterfeit: the contents did not match the label.[^7]
- **37%** were substandard: the right substance in an amount outside the accepted range. Two thirds of those were under strength.[^7]
- Among the counterfeits, 24% contained no active ingredient and 44% contained a different substance.[^7]

The authors write that these proportions "must be interpreted with caution" because the studies differed so much.[^7] Even so, no buyer can assume the label is right. Our article on [underground lab vs pharmaceutical-grade steroids](/blog/best-underground-labs-vs-pharmaceutical) sets out these findings and other analyses in more detail.

Enforcement tells the same story. In March 2026, INTERPOL's Operation Pangea XVIII ran across 90 countries and territories. Anabolic steroids were "once again the dominant category" among performance and lifestyle medicines, with 86,732 doses seized, and the operation disrupted approximately 5,700 criminal-linked websites, social media pages, channels and automated bots.[^8]

### Rogue online sellers

The US National Association of Boards of Pharmacy (NABP) reviews websites that sell prescription medicines to US patients. As of 30 June 2018, it had reviewed 11,943 of them and found 11,324 (94.8%) operating out of compliance with state and federal laws or its patient safety and pharmacy practice standards.[^9] Among the sites on its Not Recommended list:

- 89% did not require a valid prescription.[^9]
- 63% posted no location on the website.[^9]
- 17% did not have secure sites, "exposing customers to financial fraud and identity theft".[^9]

Those figures are from 2018 and cover prescription medicines in general, not steroids alone.

The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) warns that unsafe online pharmacies "often sell unapproved, counterfeit or otherwise unsafe medicines". What they send may "Have too much or too little of the active ingredient", may "Not contain the right active ingredient", or may "Contain the wrong ingredients or other harmful substances".[^10] The FDA's checklist adds two financial warnings: a site that "Charges you for products you never ordered or received", and one that "Does not provide clear written protections of your personal and financial information".[^11]

### Payment scams

How you pay decides what you can do when something goes wrong. The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) says "Credit cards and debit cards have legal protections if something goes wrong", while "Cryptocurrency payments do not come with legal protections" and "typically are not reversible".[^12] It also warns that scammers "often insist that you can only pay by using cryptocurrency, wiring money through a company like MoneyGram or Western Union, using a payment app, or putting money on a gift card".[^13]

The UK's medicines regulator, the MHRA, lists "Payment accepted in crypto currencies" among its ten signs of a dodgy website. Another sign on the same list: "You are asked not to name the medicine or website in your banking transaction reference".[^14]

## What you can check about any seller

None of the checks below proves that a product is genuine. What they do is separate sellers you can hold to account from sellers you cannot.

### A real business you can reach

The FDA's signs of a safe online pharmacy include a physical address and telephone number in the United States, a licensed pharmacist to answer your questions, and a licence from a state board of pharmacy.[^11] The MHRA lists "No physical or street address available" as a warning sign.[^14] In the EU, the Falsified Medicines Directive introduced "a common logo for websites of legally-operating online pharmacies/retailers".[^15]

Be clear about what this means for steroids. A shop that sells them without a prescription, as this one does, is not a licensed pharmacy and will not appear on any pharmacy register. That check cannot be passed. What is left is whether the business is real and reachable: a contact address that gets an answer, a phone or messaging route a person replies to, and a name you can search. The FTC suggests searching the company name with the words "complaint" or "scam". It also warns against relying on star ratings, because "some reviews and ratings are fake or misleading".[^16]

### Published returns and delivery terms

A seller that will not say what happens when a parcel is lost or a product is wrong has already given you its answer. The FTC advises checking the refund policy before you buy: who pays to send an item back, how many days you have, and whether there are restocking fees.[^16] Read the delivery terms too. The MHRA lists a "Promise of quick 'next day' delivery" as a warning sign for medicines websites.[^14] Our own terms are on our [delivery](/delivery) and [returns](/legal/returns) pages. Hold them to the same standard.

### Batch numbers and a certificate you can verify

Check that the pack carries a printed batch or lot number and an expiry date, and that they match on the box, the container and any certificate. If a seller offers a certificate of analysis, find the laboratory yourself rather than through the seller's link, confirm the test is on its accredited scope, and confirm the batch number is yours. [How to read a certificate of analysis](/blog/how-to-read-a-certificate-of-analysis) walks through each step, and [how to spot counterfeit steroids](/blog/how-to-spot-counterfeit-steroids) covers packaging.

Packaging only goes so far. The two largest groups of counterfeits in the meta-analysis contained a different substance or no active ingredient at all.[^7] A vial like that can look exactly like the real thing. Only a laboratory can tell what is in it, which is why the kind of evidence a seller offers matters more than how it is presented.

### How your payment is protected

Before you pay any seller, find out what happens if the parcel never arrives. The FTC says paying by credit card "best protects you and your money in case of a scam, or if something else goes wrong".[^16] Cryptocurrency payments, the FTC says, "typically are not reversible".[^12] If you plan to pay by bank transfer or a payment app, ask your bank or the app's provider what protection you have before you pay, not after.

### A price that makes sense

The FDA lists "deep discounts or prices that seem too good to be true" as a sign of an unsafe online pharmacy.[^11] A low price does not prove a product is fake, and a high one does not prove it is real. It is one signal among several.

## Which steroid suppliers are legit?

Measured against the law, the legitimate supplier of an anabolic steroid is a licensed pharmacy dispensing it on a valid prescription. In the United States, a prescription controlled substance may not be supplied over the internet without one.[^2] A shop that sells without a prescription, this one included, is outside that system, and no regulator has checked what it sells.

When buyers ask which suppliers are legit, they usually mean something narrower: which sellers are real, deliver what they promise, and sell what the label says. The checks above can help with the first two. The third cannot be judged from outside. Forum reputations, "top supplier" rankings and review pages are not evidence, because nobody can verify who wrote them, and the FTC warns that reviews can be fake.[^16] We do not publish a list of recommended sellers or labs, and we would treat any site that does with caution.

## Red flags before you pay

- No street address, no phone number, and no way to reach a person.[^11][^14]
- Payment only by cryptocurrency, a wire transfer service, a payment app or a gift card.[^13]
- A request to leave the product or website name out of your bank reference.[^14]
- Claims such as "100% safe", "no side effects" or "quick results".[^14]
- Prices that seem too good to be true.[^11]
- A certificate of analysis that does not name your batch, or that comes from a laboratory you cannot find on your own.

- Stop using the product. Keep the packaging, the batch number and your proof of payment.
- If you paid by card for something that never arrived or was not what you ordered, contact your card issuer quickly. In the United States, the FTC says to follow up in writing within 60 days of the first bill that shows the error.[^16]
