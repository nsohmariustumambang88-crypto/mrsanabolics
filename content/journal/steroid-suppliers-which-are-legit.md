# Steroid Suppliers: Which Are Legit? How to Identify Real vs. Fake

Counterfeit steroids are a $1+ billion/year problem. 60-80% of black market testosterone is fake. This guide teaches you how to identify legitimate suppliers and avoid scams.

## Red Flags: Illegitimate Suppliers

### 🚩 Website Red Flags
- **Brand new domain** (under 6 months old) — scammers launch sites that disappear after collection
- **Spelling/grammar errors** — legitimate suppliers proofread
- **No physical address** — scammers hide location
- **Gmail/Yahoo email** — scammers use free email; legit use business email
- **Social media age** — Instagram account created 2 weeks ago = scam

### 🚩 Business Red Flags
- **No way to contact** — phone number doesn't work, emails ignored
- **Only accepts wire transfer** — money sent can't be reversed
- **Pressure to pay quickly** — "Only 5 doses left!" creates urgency
- **No customer reviews** — scammers delete negative reviews or have none

### 🚩 Product Red Flags
- **No Certificate of Analysis** — legitimate suppliers provide CoAs
- **Same CoA for every batch** — real batches have different CoA numbers
- **Prices too low** — testosterone under $30/month = fake
- **Vague product descriptions** — real suppliers specify dosage/volume clearly

### 🚩 Shipping Red Flags
- **No tracking** — can't verify package is on the way
- **Slow shipping** — takes 4+ weeks (normal is 1-2 weeks)
- **Vague shipping location** — "ships from overseas" = risky

## Green Flags: Legitimate Suppliers

### ✅ Website Legitimacy
- **Domain age 5+ years** — scammers don't stay around long
- **Professional website design** — legitimate operation invests in appearance
- **Detailed contact information** — business address, phone, email all available
- **Business email address** — company@domain.com not gmail
- **SSL certificate** — https:// connection

### ✅ Business Legitimacy
- **Responsive communication** — answers emails within 24 hours
- **Verifiable business registration** — company searchable in business registry
- **Multiple payment options** — offers crypto AND bank transfer (flexibility)
- **Customer reviews online** — positive AND negative reviews visible (unvetted pages)
- **Years in business** — 5+ years of consistent operation

### ✅ Product Legitimacy
- **Certificate of Analysis provided** — CoA available upon request
- **Different CoAs per batch** — each batch number has unique CoA
- **Realistic pricing** — testosterone $50-150/month (matches market)
- **Clear specifications** — product name, dosage, volume all clear
- **Professional packaging** — branded labels, sealed vials, professional appearance

### ✅ Shipping Legitimacy
- **Tracking provided** — package can be tracked until delivery
- **Discreet packaging** — doesn't advertise contents
- **Reasonable timeframe** — 1-2 weeks domestic, 2-4 weeks international
- **Shipping insurance** — reimburses if package lost

## How to Verify a Supplier

### Step 1: Research Business Registration (5 minutes)

```
Google: "[Company Name] business registration"

Search in:
- State Secretary of State database (US)
- Companies House (UK)
- ASIC (Australia)
- Equivalent registry for country
```

**What to look for:**
- Company exists and is registered
- Business address is real (check Google Maps)
- Registration date (5+ years = more trustworthy)
- No lawsuit history visible

---

### Step 2: Verify Certificate of Analysis (15 minutes)

```
1. Get CoA from supplier
2. Note lab name (e.g., "GLM Labs")
3. Google "[Lab Name] accreditation"
4. Find lab independently on ILAC.org or A2LA.org
5. Call lab: "Did you test batch [number] on [date]?"
6. Lab confirms or denies
```

**What to expect:**
- Accredited lab will have records
- Batch number matches CoA
- Test date within 3-6 months
- Results show testosterone concentration

**Red flag:**
- Lab won't confirm they issued the CoA = FAKE

---

### Step 3: Check Community Reviews (10 minutes)

```
Search: "[Supplier name] review"
Search: "[Supplier name] reddit"
Search: "[Brand name] forum"

Look for:
- Reviews from past 6 months (old reviews irrelevant)
- Specific results reported (not generic praise)
- Both positive AND negative reviews
- Detailed accounts of experience
```

**Red flags:**
- All reviews are glowing with no criticism
- Reviews are generic ("Great service!")
- No reviews at all (new supplier)
- Reviews older than 1 year

---

### Step 4: Price Sanity Check (2 minutes)

| Product | Realistic Cost |
|---------|----------------|
| Testosterone 250mg/ml | $50-150/month |
| Nandrolone 300mg/ml | $80-180/month |
| Trenbolone 100mg/ml | $80-200/month |
| Dianabol tabs (50mg) | $30-80/month |

**Rule:** If price is 50%+ below this range, product is fake.

## How Counterfeit Steroids Are Made

### Scenario 1: Under-dosed
- Supplier fills vial with mostly oil (cheap filler)
- Adds minimal testosterone (20% actual dose)
- User gets 50mg testosterone instead of 250mg
- Cost: $5 to produce, sells for $100

### Scenario 2: Wrong substance entirely
- Vial labeled "testosterone" contains vegetable oil
- Or contains completely different steroid
- Cost to produce: $3, sells for $100
- Detection: Only found after bloodwork or zero results

### Scenario 3: Contaminated
- Steroid made in unsanitary lab
- Contains bacterial/fungal contaminants
- User injects infection, gets abscess
- Risk: Medical emergency

## Supplier Tiers (Ranked by Reliability)

### Tier 1: Pharmaceutical (100% Legitimate)
- Source: Licensed manufacturer
- Quality: Guaranteed
- Verification: Pharmacy receipt
- Cost: $150-300/month
- Risk: Zero
- Access: Prescription required

**Examples:**
- Watson Laboratories
- Actavis
- Pfizer

- Source: Underground lab with 5+ years history
- Quality: Generally consistent

- Cost: $80-150/month
- Risk: Low-moderate (counterfeits exist)
- Access: Black market

**Examples:**
- Geneza Pharmaceuticals

- Source: New underground lab
- Quality: Unknown
- Verification: Limited reviews
- Cost: Variable
- Risk: High (may be counterfeit)
- Access: Black market

**Recommendation:** Avoid unless desperate

---

### Tier 4: Street Level (0-10% Reliable)
- Source: Unknown
- Quality: Unknown
- Verification: None
- Cost: Variable
- Risk: Extremely high
- Access: Street/gym

**Recommendation:** Never buy

## What to Do If You Get Fake Steroids

**Option 1: Bloodwork Verification**
- Get blood test 3-4 weeks into cycle
- Check testosterone level
- If normal range (300-700): Fake steroid
- If elevated (1000+): Real steroid

**Option 2: Try Another Source**
- Don't confront scammer (not safe)
- Note supplier name and avoid
- Find new source with verified reviews
- Money is lost (no recourse in black market)

**Option 3: Lab Test the Product**
- Send sample to private lab (Jano, Simec)
- Costs $100-200 but identifies product
- Proves if fake or misrepresented

## Summary

**How to identify legit suppliers:**
1. Check business registration (5 year minimum)
2. Verify Certificate of Analysis independently
3. Read community reviews (past 6 months)
4. Compare price to market rate
5. Research supplier reputation

**Biggest red flags:**
- No way to contact
- No CoA available
- Unrealistic pricing
- No community reviews
- Website under 1 year old

**Best practice:** Spend 30 minutes verifying before ordering. Money saved is not worth the risk of counterfeit/contaminated product.
