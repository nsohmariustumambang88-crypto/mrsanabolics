# Best Steroids for Beginners: A Complete Guide for First-Time Users

Choosing the right steroid for a first cycle is the most critical decision a beginner will make. The wrong choice leads to unnecessary side effects, wasted money, and discouragement. This guide explains which steroids are safest, most effective, and most forgiving for your first cycle. For a complete beginner's cycle blueprint, [see our steroid cycle guide](/blog/steroid-cycle-guide-beginners).

## What Makes a Steroid "Good" for Beginners?

Before listing specific compounds, understand what separates beginner-friendly steroids from dangerous ones.

### Low Androgenic Side Effects
Androgenic effects include acne, hair loss, prostate enlargement, and mood swings. Beginners should avoid compounds that are heavily androgenic because their bodies are not adapted to high hormone levels yet.

### Predictable Effects
Some steroids have variable effects between individuals. Beginners need compounds where the results are consistent and well-understood — so you know what to expect.

### Reversible Side Effects
All steroid side effects are not permanent. Beginners should choose compounds where [side effects resolve quickly after stopping](/blog/steroid-side-effects-complete-guide), not ones that cause lasting damage.

### Mild Virilization Risk
If female, virilization (development of male characteristics) is a serious concern. Some steroids are safer than others.

### Affordable
Beginner cycles don't need to cost $500+/month. Affordable compounds let you run a proper dose without financial strain.

## The Best Steroids for Beginners (Ranked)

### 1. Testosterone Enanthate (The Gold Standard)

**Why it's #1:** Every person's body naturally produces testosterone. Exogenous testosterone is simply adding more of what you already have. This makes it the safest and most forgiving first steroid.

**Dosage:** 300-500 mg/week (beginners should start low)
**Duration:** 12 weeks (standard first cycle)
**Effect Timeline:**
- Week 1-2: Mood elevation, motivation boost
- Week 3-4: Visible strength gains
- Week 5-8: Noticeable muscle gain
- Week 9-12: Peak effects, significant size/strength

**Side Effects:**
- Water retention (manageable)
- Acne (mild-moderate, individual variation)
- Gynecomastia risk (use AI if needed)
- Shut down natural production (recovers post-cycle)

**Cost:** $50-100/month (affordable)
**Beginner Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Why Beginners Choose It:**
- Results are PROVEN and consistent
- Side effects are mild compared to other compounds
- Well-studied (decades of data)
- Recovery is straightforward
- You can run it long-term if needed

### 2. Testosterone Propionate (Fast Acting, Safer to Stop)

**Why it's great:** Same benefits as enanthate, but clears your system in 3 days instead of 10. If sides appear, you can stop and recover in a week.

**Dosage:** 100 mg/day or 350 mg/week
**Duration:** 8-12 weeks
**Effect Timeline:** Same as enanthate, just more frequent injections

**Drawback:** Requires daily or every-other-day injections (more needles)

**Beginner Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (very good, just more injections)

---

### 3. Nandrolone (Deca-Durabolin) — Advanced Beginners Only

**Why it's good:** Highly anabolic, low androgenic effects, benefits joints/collagen.

**Dosage:** 300-400 mg/week
**Duration:** 12-16 weeks
**Combined with:** Testosterone 300 mg/week (cannot run Deca alone)

**Drawback:** Slower acting (takes 4-5 weeks to feel), can cause water retention and "deca dick" (erectile dysfunction).

**Beginner Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐ (good but more complex)

**Best for:** Beginners prioritizing joint health, or those doing heavy compound lifts with existing joint pain.

---

### 4. Boldenone (EQ, Equipoise) — The Lean Gainer

**Why it's good:** Slow-acting, clean gains without water, good for building lean muscle.

**Dosage:** 400-600 mg/week
**Duration:** 12-16 weeks
**Combined with:** Testosterone 300 mg/week

**Effect Timeline:**
- Week 1-4: Minimal (long-acting)
- Week 5-8: Noticeable appetite increase, strength gains
- Week 9-12: Muscle definition, lean gains
- Peak effect: Week 12-16

**Beginner Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (excellent, but slow)

**Best for:** Beginners who want clean, lean gains without bloating.

---

### 5. Anavar (Oxandrolone) — The Mild Option

**Why it's good:** Mildest anabolic steroid available. Safe for females. Doesn't shut down testosterone as severely.

**Dosage (Male):** 50-100 mg/day
**Dosage (Female):** 10-25 mg/day
**Duration:** 8-12 weeks
**Cost:** EXPENSIVE ($150-300/month)

**Drawback:** Expensive and mild effects. Better as a finisher to a cycle than a standalone.

**Beginner Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐ (very safe, but expensive and mild)

**Best for:** Females starting steroids, or males wanting maximum safety.

## What Beginners Should AVOID

### ❌ Trenbolone
Extremely potent. Even experienced users sometimes regret running tren on their first cycle. Side effects are severe. No beginner should touch it.

### ❌ Anadrol (Oxymetholone)
Causes extreme water retention, liver stress, and strong androgenic sides. Too harsh for beginners.

### ❌ Dianabol (Methandrostenolone)
Oral steroid with severe liver toxicity. Results are mostly water. Not worth the risk.

### ❌ Winstrol (Stanozolol)
Causes joint pain and is harsh on the liver. Beginners have no reason to use it.

### ❌ Halotestin
Extremely androgenic. Only experienced users who understand the risks should consider it.

### ❌ Multiple Steroids on First Cycle
Adding more compounds doesn't guarantee better results — it guarantees more side effects. Master one compound first.

## Beginner Training Requirements

**A steroid is only as good as your training.**

Your first cycle will only work if you:
- Train with progressive overload (increase weight/reps weekly)
- Hit each muscle 2x/week
- Eat 0.8-1g protein per lb bodyweight
- Sleep 7-9 hours nightly
- Run cycle for 12+ weeks (short cycles waste the money)

A beginner running test without proper training will still gain muscle, but will waste 40% of the steroid's potential.

## Post-Cycle Therapy (PCT)

After your cycle ends, natural testosterone is suppressed for weeks. PCT jumpstarts recovery.

### Standard PCT (Weeks 13-17)

**Clomid Option:**
- Weeks 13-15: 50 mg/day
- Weeks 16-17: 25 mg/day

**OR Nolvadex Option:**
- Weeks 13-15: 20 mg/day
- Weeks 16-17: 10 mg/day

**Goal:** Restore LH/FSH signaling so testes resume testosterone production.

**Without PCT:** Recovery takes 8-12 weeks. With PCT: Recovery takes 4-6 weeks.
