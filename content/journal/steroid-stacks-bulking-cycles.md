# Steroid Stacks for Bulking: 12 Complete Cycles for Muscle Gain

This guide provides complete, ready-to-run bulking cycles from beginner to advanced. Each cycle includes compound selection, dosages, duration, expected results, and cost.

[For detailed information on individual compounds, see our steroid guides](/blog/best-steroids-for-muscle-gain).

## Why Stack Steroids?

Single-compound cycles are less effective than stacks because:
1. **Synergy** — Two steroids at moderate doses often outperform one at high dose
2. **Lower sides** — Distributed dose means fewer side effects per compound
3. **Joint health** — Stacking nandrolone provides collagen/joint benefits
4. **Flexibility** — Can address multiple goals (mass + strength + joint)

## STACK 1: Test Only (Classic Beginner)

**For:** Absolute beginners, first steroid cycle

```

Week 1-12: Testosterone Enanthate 500 mg/week (250mg Mon + 250mg Thu)

Week 13-15: PCT (Clomid 50mg/day OR Nolvadex 20mg/day)

Estimated Results:
- Muscle gain: 15-25 lbs
- Body fat change: -2-5% (slight loss)
- Strength increase: +50-100 lbs on major lifts
- Side effects: Mild (water retention, acne risk)

Cost: $200-300 total ($50-100/month testosterone)

Why this stack:
- Simple (one injection 2x weekly)
- Proven results (testosterone is gold standard)
- Lower side effects than multi-compound stacks
- Good learning curve for managing hormones
- Recovery is straightforward

Note: [See beginner steroid recommendations](/blog/best-steroids-for-beginners)
```

## General Bulking Stack Principles

**Apply to any stack:**

1. **Testosterone is the foundation** — Every stack needs test (minimum 200mg/week)
2. **Higher total volume = more results** — 600mg total compounds > 300mg total
3. **Longer cycles = better utilization** — 16-week cycles yield better ROI than 8-week
4. **Synergy matters** — Stacks outperform single compounds at same total dose
5. **Nutrition must support the cycle** — +500-1000 cals surplus required
6. **Training must be intense** — Steroids amplify training, not replace it
7. **Sleep is critical** — 7-9 hours nightly for optimal gains

## Stack Selection by Experience Level

**Complete Beginner (First Cycle Ever):**
- Start with Test Only
- Learn how your body responds
- Establish baseline side effect profile

**Intermediate (2-3 Previous Cycles):**
- Progress to Test + Deca or Test + EQ
- Add complexity gradually
- Master one stack before moving to next

**Advanced (5+ Cycles):**
- Run more aggressive stacks (Tren-containing)
- Higher total doses (600-900mg/week)
- More compounds simultaneously
- Shorter, higher-intensity cycles
