# How to Test Steroids for Authenticity: Complete Verification Guide

## Visual Inspection (Free, Basic)

### 1. Vial Appearance
**Real vials have:**
- Professional labeling (clear, color-matched to brand)
- Batch numbers matching Certificate of Analysis
- Proper expiration dates
- Sealed caps (not loose or damaged)
- Oil clarity (testosterone is clear to amber, not cloudy)

**Fake vials have:**
- Blurry or hand-written labels
- Batch numbers missing or don't match CoA
- Expired dates
- Loose/damaged seals
- Cloudy oil (bacterial contamination)

### 2. Oil Color & Clarity
- **Testosterone:** Clear to light amber
- **Nandrolone:** Clear to slight amber
- **Boldenone:** Clear to light amber
- **Trenbolone:** Pale yellow to amber

**Red flags:**
- Cloudy or opaque (contamination)
- Very dark brown (oxidized/degraded)
- Separation/settling (improper storage)

### 3. Vial Weight
Counterfeit vials are often lighter (less oil = less cost to fake).

- Take a legitimate vial and a suspect vial
- Compare weight (should be identical)
- Significantly lighter = likely fake

## Blood Test (Practical Proof)

**Cost:** $100-200
**What it shows:** Testosterone level confirms steroid is real or fake

**How:**
1. Get baseline testosterone before cycle (natural level)
2. Inject steroid for 3-4 weeks
3. Get blood test (should show elevated testosterone)
4. Compare to baseline:
   - If elevated (>1000 ng/dL): Real steroid
   - If normal or low: Fake steroid

**Accuracy:** High (direct measurement)
**Problem:** You're already injecting before confirming authenticity

## Chemistry Tests (Home, Basic)

### Melting Point Test
- Heat steroid powder to melting point
- Compare to known melting point for that steroid
- Different melting point = likely fake

**Problem:** Not precise without equipment

### Color Reaction Tests
- Mix steroid with reagents (chemistry compounds)
- Observe color change
- Different color = different substance

**Reagents needed:**
- Sulfuric acid
- Nitric acid
- Others depending on steroid

**Problem:** Dangerous (chemicals are corrosive), requires chemistry knowledge

## Red Flag Testing Method

**What combination indicates fake:**

| Test | Result | Interpretation |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| Appearance | Cloudy oil | Contaminated/fake |
| Weight | Significantly lighter | Likely fake (less oil) |
| Vial seal | Broken/loose | Compromised |
| Label | Blurry/hand-written | Likely fake |
| Batch number | Missing/wrong | Fake |
| All of above | Multiple red flags | Almost certainly fake |

**One red flag:** Suspicious but not definitive
**Two+ red flags:** Likely counterfeit — don't inject

## Bloodwork Interpretation

### Normal Testosterone Baseline (Natural)
- Adult male: 300-700 ng/dL
- Female: 15-70 ng/dL

### After Testosterone Cycle (Real steroid)
- Male at 400 mg/week: 1200-2000 ng/dL
- Male at 600 mg/week: 1800-3000+ ng/dL

### After Fake Steroid
- No change from baseline
- Testosterone stays 300-700 ng/dL

**The test:** If you don't see elevation after 3-4 weeks, steroid is fake.

## Certificate of Analysis Verification

**Get the CoA from supplier:**
1. Note lab name
2. Google lab independently
3. Verify lab accreditation
4. Call lab: "Did you test batch [number]?"
5. Lab confirms or denies

**If lab denies testing the batch:** CoA is forged
