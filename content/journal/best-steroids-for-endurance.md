# Best Steroids for Endurance: Performance Enhancement Guide

Endurance athletes — distance runners, cyclists, rowers, swimmers — have different steroid priorities than strength athletes. Endurance compounds increase red blood cell production, oxygen delivery, and aerobic capacity. This guide explains which steroids maximize endurance performance.

## How Steroids Improve Endurance

**Endurance = oxygen delivery to muscles**

The limiting factor in aerobic performance is oxygen. Your lungs deliver oxygen through blood to muscles. Muscles use oxygen to produce energy (ATP). Endurance steroids increase this pipeline:

1. **Increased Red Blood Cells (Erythropoiesis)** — more oxygen carriers
2. **Increased Hemoglobin** — more oxygen per RBC
3. **Improved Cardiovascular Function** — better blood flow
4. **Mitochondrial Density** — more cellular energy factories
5. **Lactate Buffering** — delay fatigue onset

## The Science: Red Blood Cells and Oxygen

**Normal blood values:**
- RBC count: 4.5-6.0 million/mm³
- Hemoglobin: 13-17 g/dL (males)
- Hematocrit: 38-50% (percentage of blood that is RBCs)

**On endurance steroids:**
- RBC count: 6.0-8.0 million/mm³ (elevated)
- Hemoglobin: 18-19 g/dL (elevated)
- Hematocrit: 52-60% (elevated)

**What this means:** More oxygen carrying capacity = 10-20% performance boost.

## Training for Endurance on Steroids

**Endurance training doesn't change on steroids — it just becomes more effective.**

### Base Building Phase (Weeks 1-4)
- 60-80% of training at easy pace
- Long run/ride: 90-120 min at conversational pace
- 2-3x/week aerobic base work

### Threshold Phase (Weeks 5-8)
- 70% easy, 30% threshold
- Threshold work: 3x8-10 min at hard pace
- Still mostly aerobic base

### Specificity Phase (Weeks 9-12)
- Sport-specific training
- Race-pace efforts
- VO2 max work (if applicable)
- Taper and recovery

**Why this matters:** Steroids amplify training. Better training × steroids = better results. Poor training × steroids = mediocre results.

## Risks Specific to Endurance Athletes

### Blood Viscosity (Thickness)
High RBC count thickens blood, increasing cardiovascular strain and clot risk.

**Mitigation:**
- Regular blood work (check hematocrit)
- Hydration (stay above 3L water/day)
- Don't use EPO (extreme viscosity risk)
- Stop steroid if hematocrit exceeds 55%

### Cardiovascular Stress
Endurance + high RBCs = huge cardiac workload.

**Mitigation:**
- Monitor blood pressure
- Avoid excessive doses
- Don't combine with high-intensity training initially
