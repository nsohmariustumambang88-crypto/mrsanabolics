## The Science: How Cutting Steroids Work

### Increased Fat Oxidation
Trenbolone and other cutting steroids increase the rate your body burns fat for energy. This is why you lose fat faster on the same calorie deficit.

### Muscle-Sparing Effect
On a diet, your body breaks down muscle for energy. Cutting steroids tell your body to break down fat instead. This prevents the muscle loss that occurs with plain dieting.

### Increased Metabolic Rate
Cutting steroids raise your baseline calorie expenditure. A 200 lb male on tren burns ~300-400 more calories/day than baseline — even at rest.

### Appetite Suppression (Some)
Trenbolone and masteron can reduce appetite, making it easier to stay in a calorie deficit without constant hunger.

## Cutting Diet Requirements

**Calorie deficit needed:** -500 calories/day (lose ~1 lb/week)
**Protein requirement:** 1.0-1.2 g per lb (critical to preserve muscle)
**Carbs:** 1.5-2 g per lb bodyweight
**Fats:** 0.3-0.4 g per lb (minimum for hormones)

**Example (200 lb person cutting):**
- Maintenance: 3000 calories/day
- Cutting calories: 2500 calories/day
- Protein: 200-240 g (800-960 cals)
- Carbs: 300-400 g (1200-1600 cals)
- Fats: 60-80 g (540-720 cals)

## Training During a Cut

**Goal:** Maintain strength and muscle (not build)

**Best training split:**
- 4-5 days/week (maintain volume)
- Compound focus (bench, squat, deadlift, rows)
- Lower reps (4-6 range, maintain strength)
- Fewer isolation exercises (preserve recovery capacity)

**Example cutting workout:**
- Day 1: Chest/Shoulders (bench 4x5, OHP 3x5, rows)
- Day 2: Legs (squat 4x5, leg press 3x8, leg curl 3x8)
- Day 3: Rest or light cardio
- Day 4: Back/Shoulders (deadlift 3x5, pull-ups 3x6, OHP 3x5)
- Day 5: Accessory (arms, calves, isolation)
- Days 6-7: Rest

---

## Cardio During a Cut

**Best cardio for steroid cuts:**
- LISS (low-intensity steady state): 30-45 min moderate pace, 3-4x/week
- HIIT: 15-20 min intense intervals, 1-2x/week
- Walking: 45-60 min daily (burns calories, low recovery cost)

**Why avoid excessive cardio:**
- Eats muscle tissue
- Increases recovery cost
- Reduces training performance
- Steroids already maximize fat loss (excess cardio unnecessary)
