# Aromatase Inhibitor (AI) Guide: Managing Estrogen on Steroids

Aromatase Inhibitors lower estrogen levels during steroid cycles. This guide covers AI selection, dosing, and side effects.

[See our gynecomastia prevention guide](/blog/steroid-gynecomastia-prevention-treatment).

## How AIs Work

AIs block aromatase enzyme (converts testosterone to estrogen). Result: Lower estrogen.

**Steroids that aromatize:**
- Testosterone (heavily)
- Nandrolone (moderately)
- Dianabol (heavily)
- Boldenone (slightly)

**Steroids that don't:**
- Trenbolone
- Anavar
- Winstrol
- Masteron

## AI Types

### Anastrozole (Arimidex)

**Dosing:** 0.25-0.5mg daily (or every other day)
**Cost:** $20-40 for full cycle
**Onset:** 24-48 hours
**Timeline:** 72 hours for full effect

**Sides:** Joint pain, dry skin, mood issues (if overdosed)

### Letrozole (Femara)

**Dosing:** 0.5-1.25mg daily
**Cost:** $30-50 for full cycle
**Onset:** Faster/more powerful than anastrozole
**Timeline:** 24-48 hours for full effect

**Sides:** More pronounced low-estrogen sides than anastrozole

### Exemestane (Aromasin)

**Dosing:** 12.5-25mg daily
**Cost:** $25-45 for full cycle
**Onset:** Similar to anastrozole
**Timeline:** 48-72 hours

**Advantage:** Slightly fewer joint-pain sides

## AI Dosing Strategy

### Preventive (Start Day 1)

**Anastrozole:** 0.25-0.5mg daily
**Letrozole:** 0.5-1mg daily
**Exemestane:** 12.5mg daily

**Why start early:** Prevent gyno before it starts

### Responsive (Start When Gyno Appears)

**Anastrozole:** 0.5-1mg daily
**Letrozole:** 1-1.25mg daily
**Exemestane:** 25mg daily

**Timeline:** 3-7 days to see reduction in puffiness

### Low-Dose Cycles

**If running low testosterone (200-300mg):**
- AI may not be needed
- OR use very low dose: 0.25mg anastrozole every other day

**Reason:** Lower test = less aromatization

## AI Side Effects

### Joint Pain (Most Common)

**Why:** Estrogen is needed for joint lubrication

**Management:**
- Lower AI dose
- Supplement with glucosamine/collagen
- Accept mild joint pain as tradeoff
- Use Nolvadex instead (blocks without lowering)

### Dry Skin

**Why:** Estrogen supports skin hydration

**Management:**
- Moisturize daily
- Hydrate (drink water)
- Lower AI dose if severe

### Mood Issues / Depression

**Why:** Low estrogen affects mood regulation

**Management:**
- Lower AI dose
- Ensure adequate food intake
- May resolve after cycle

### Cardiovascular Issues

**Why:** Estrogen protects cardiovascular health

**Management:**
- Don't overdo AI (keep estrogen in range, not zero)
- Cardio 3-4x weekly
- Monitor blood pressure

### Low Libido / ED (Paradoxical)

**Why:** Despite blocking estrogen, sometimes happens from overall hormonal disruption

**Management:**
- Paradoxical — lowering AI may help
- Ensure testosterone is adequate
- Check for low estrogen (too much AI)

## Estrogen Target Ranges

**Optimal during cycle:** 20-30 pg/mL

**Below 10:** Too low (sides: joints, mood, libido issues)
**10-20:** Slight suppression (generally OK)
**20-30:** Ideal
**30-40:** Slightly high (gyno risk beginning)
**40+:** Too high (active gynecomastia risk)

**Get blood work:** Check estradiol levels mid-cycle (week 4-6)

## AI + Testosterone Ratio

Higher test = more aromatization = more AI needed

**Examples:**
- Test 300mg/week → 0.25mg anastrozole daily
- Test 500mg/week → 0.5mg anastrozole daily
- Test 750mg/week → 0.75-1mg anastrozole daily

## AI During Different Compounds

### Testosterone + Nandrolone
- AI needed (both aromatize)
- Normal dosing: 0.25-0.5mg anastrozole daily

### Testosterone + Trenbolone
- Low AI needed (tren doesn't aromatize)
- Dosing: 0.25mg every other day (or none)

### Testosterone + Anavar + Masteron
- Low-moderate AI (anavar/masteron don't aromatize, test does)
- Dosing: 0.25mg every other day

## AI + Nolvadex Comparison

**Should you use both?**

**AI (lowers estrogen):**
- Good for testosterone cycles (prevent gyno from aromatization)
- Bad: Can cause low-estrogen sides

**Nolvadex (blocks estrogen reception):**
- Good for nandrolone cycles (doesn't aromatize much, but progestin effects)
- Good: Doesn't lower estrogen (fewer sides)
- Bad: Takes longer to work

**Recommendation:**
- Test cycles: Use AI
- Nandrolone cycles: Use Nolvadex
- Combo cycles: Use AI primarily, add Nolvadex if needed

## Stopping AI Post-Cycle

**When to stop:**
- When steroids stop (estrogen levels normalize on their own during PCT)
- Don't continue AI during PCT (need estrogen for recovery)

**Timeline:**
- Last dose of AI: Same day as last steroid injection (approximately)
- AI clears in 24-48 hours (no need to taper)

## AI Myths Debunked

**Myth:** "More AI = better results"
- Truth: Lower estrogen doesn't increase muscle gain. In fact, too-low estrogen may impair gains.

**Myth:** "Skip AI if using nandrolone (doesn't aromatize much)"
- Truth: Use Nolvadex instead (nandrolone has progestin effects).

**Myth:** "AI will eliminate gyno completely"
- Truth: AI prevents gyno; for existing gyno in later stages, surgery may be needed.
