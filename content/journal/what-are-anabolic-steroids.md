# What Are Anabolic Steroids? Complete Explanation

Anabolic steroids are synthetic drugs that mimic testosterone and promote muscle growth. Understanding what they are, how they work, and what they do requires separating scientific fact from marketing claims and myths.

## Definition and Chemical Structure

Anabolic steroids are **androgenic-anabolic steroids (AAS)** — synthetic compounds derived from or designed to mimic testosterone, the male sex hormone naturally produced in the body.

**Key characteristics:**
- **Synthetic** (laboratory-created, not extracted from natural sources)
- **Derived from testosterone** (modified chemical structure)
- **Androgenic** (produce male sexual characteristics)
- **Anabolic** (promote muscle/tissue growth)

The "anabolic" and "androgenic" effects cannot be completely separated. Modifying the chemical structure can increase the anabolic effect relative to the androgenic effect, but neither can be eliminated entirely.

## How They Differ from Testosterone

**Testosterone** (the natural hormone):
- Produced naturally in testes and adrenal glands
- Regulates muscle, bone, mood, sexual function
- Produced in cycles (higher in morning, varies seasonally)
- Metabolized quickly (half-life ~10 minutes in blood)

**Anabolic steroids** (synthetic versions):
- Chemically modified testosterone
- Modified to be more resistant to metabolism (longer-acting)
- Modified to increase anabolic vs. androgenic effects
- Can be designed for different routes (oral, injectable)

**Example:** Nandrolone (Deca-Durabolin) is testosterone with a modified structure that increases anabolic effect (~2.5x more anabolic than testosterone) while decreasing androgenic effect (~0.4x less androgenic).

## Types of Anabolic Steroids

### By Route of Administration

For a detailed comparison, [read our guide on oral vs. injectable steroids](/blog/oral-vs-injectable-steroids).

**Injectable steroids:**
- Dissolved in oil and injected intramuscularly
- Absorbed slowly from the injection site
- Last 7-30+ days per injection (depending on ester)
- Examples: Testosterone enanthate, Trenbolone, Nandrolone

**Oral steroids:**
- Taken by mouth like tablets or capsules
- Processed by the liver
- Last 6-24 hours (require daily dosing)
- Examples: Anavar (oxandrolone), Winstrol (stanozolol), Dianabol (methandrostenolone)

**Other routes:**
- Transdermal (patches or gels)
- Sublingual (under tongue)
- Implants (rare)

### By Chemical Class

**Testosterone esters** (modified testosterone for longer action)
- Testosterone enanthate (10-day duration)
- Testosterone cypionate (12-day duration)
- Testosterone propionate (3-day duration)
- Testosterone undecanoate (ultra-long, 25-day)

**Nandrolones** (testosterone with modified structure)
- Nandrolone decanoate (Deca-Durabolin)
- Nandrolone phenylpropionate (NPP)

**Trenbolones** (highly anabolic, minimal androgenic)
- Trenbolone acetate (3-day duration)
- Trenbolone enanthate (10-day duration)

**Boldenones** (slow-acting, steady effects)
- Boldenone undecylenate (EQ, Equipoise)

**Oral compounds**
- Oxandrolone (Anavar) — mild, well-tolerated
- Stanozolol (Winstrol) — cutting compound
- Methandrostenolone (Dianabol) — bulking compound
- Oxymetholone (Anadrol) — extreme mass gain

## What Anabolic Steroids Actually Do

For a detailed explanation of the biological mechanisms, [read how steroids work at the cellular level](/blog/how-do-steroids-work).

### Primary Effects

**Anabolic effects** (tissue-building):
- Increase protein synthesis (muscle builds faster)
- Promote muscle growth (hypertrophy)
- Increase strength and power output
- Improve recovery between workouts
- Increase red blood cell production (more oxygen)
- Enhance bone density

**Androgenic effects** (male characteristics):
- Develop/maintain male sexual characteristics
- Deepen voice
- Increase body/facial hair
- Enhance libido

### Secondary Effects

**Performance benefits:**
- Reduced fatigue during training
- Faster recovery from workouts
- Increased motivation/aggression
- Improved mood (in some individuals)
- Increased appetite (variable)

**Physical changes:**
- Muscle definition increases
- Body composition shifts (less fat, more muscle)
- Vascularity may increase
- Skin may become oily (acne risk)

## The Anabolic/Androgenic Ratio

Each steroid has a ratio comparing its anabolic effects to its androgenic effects.

**Example:**
- Testosterone = 1:1 ratio (baseline)
- Nandrolone = 2.5:0.4 (more anabolic, less androgenic)
- Trenbolone = 5:0 (extremely anabolic, minimal androgenic)

**Why it matters:**
- Higher anabolic ratio = more muscle growth, fewer male characteristics
- Ratio affects side effects (higher androgenic = more sexual side effects)
- No steroid is purely anabolic (some androgenic effect always present)

## How Steroids Affect the Body

### Mechanism of Action

1. **Steroid enters the body** (injection or ingestion)
2. **Binds to androgen receptors** on muscle cells and other tissues
3. **Activates protein synthesis** (the cell builds more protein)
4. **Muscle growth accelerates** (you gain muscle faster than naturally possible)
5. **Metabolism increases** (you burn slightly more calories)

**In practical terms:**
- Naturally, you might gain 1-2 lbs of muscle per month
- On anabolic steroids, you might gain 5-10 lbs per month
- The actual increase depends on the dose, compound, genetics, training, and nutrition

### Receptor Sensitivity

**Not all tissues respond equally:**
- Muscle tissue is very sensitive (main target)
- Prostate is sensitive (enlargement risk)
- Liver processes some compounds (stress risk)
- Hair follicles are sensitive (acceleration of male pattern baldness if genetically predisposed)

## Natural vs. Pharmaceutical vs. Underground Lab

### Pharmaceutical (Prescription)

**Source:** Licensed manufacturers (Bayer, Merck, etc.)
**Quality:** Regulated, consistent dosing
**Cost:** $100-300 per month
**Legal:** Only with valid prescription
**Availability:** Through legitimate pharmacies

**Uses:** Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT), delayed puberty, muscle-wasting diseases

### Underground Laboratory (UGL)

**Source:** Illegal manufacturers
**Quality:** Highly variable (contamination, underdose, overdose possible)
**Cost:** $50-150 per month (cheaper but riskier)
**Legal:** Illegal
**Verification:** Limited to lab testing

**Reality:** UGL quality varies dramatically. Some are as good as pharmaceutical; others contain harmful contaminants or wrong substances entirely.

### Natural Testosterone Production

**Source:** Your own body (testes in men, adrenal glands in women)
**Level:** Varies by age, time of day, health
**Male typical range:** 300-1000 ng/dL
**Cost:** Free (no intervention)

**On exogenous steroids:** Natural production shuts down (negative feedback loop). Recovery is possible but can take months after stopping.

## Historical Context

Anabolic steroids were developed in the **1930s** for medical purposes:
- 1935: First synthesized for treating hypogonadism
- 1940s: Used to treat muscle-wasting diseases
- 1950s: Athletes discovered performance benefits
- 1960s-70s: Widespread use in Olympic sports
- 1990s-2000s: Underground labs proliferate
- 2010s-present: Pharmaceutical regulations tighten, UGL market expands

## Legal Status

Anabolic steroids are **controlled substances** in most countries:

- **US:** Schedule III (felony to possess without prescription)
- **UK:** Schedule IV (illegal to supply, prosecuted for possession)
- **Canada:** Prescription-only (Schedule F)
- **Australia:** Schedule III (illegal without prescription)
- **EU:** Varies by country; generally illegal

**International trafficking** carries additional severe penalties.

## Summary: What You Need to Know

Anabolic steroids are:
- ✅ Synthetic compounds that mimic testosterone
- ✅ Highly effective at increasing muscle growth when combined with proper training/nutrition
- ✅ Capable of enhancing performance and body composition
- ✅ Variable in quality depending on source (pharmaceutical > verified UGL > unverified UGL)
- ❌ Not magic — they still require hard training and proper nutrition
- ❌ Not without side effects — all carry risks (some more than others)
- ❌ Not legal without prescription in most jurisdictions
- ❌ Not appropriate for everyone — age, health status matter

Understanding what steroids actually are, how they work, and what realistic effects are helps make informed decisions about whether they're appropriate for your goals.
