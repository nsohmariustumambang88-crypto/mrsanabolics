## Cost vs. Results

**Does cheaper = worse results?**

No. UGL compounds from established suppliers produce identical results to pharmaceutical versions IF quality is real.

**The variable:** Counterfeit vs. authentic. A fake $50/month compound produces zero results. An authentic $100/month UGL produces identical results to a $150/month pharmaceutical.

## Summary

**Most cost-effective approach:**
- Use pharmaceutical if available (TRT prescription)
- If not available, use reputable UGL (verified CoA, reviews)
- Avoid cheapest options (likely counterfeit)
- Budget $100-150/month for steroids + $500-1000/cycle for monitoring
