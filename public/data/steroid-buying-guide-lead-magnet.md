## THE 5-STEP BUYING PROCESS

### Step 1: Research (2-3 hours)
1. Find supplier through community discussion (Reddit, forums)
2. Search for independent reviews
3. Check domain registration age (WHOIS)
4. Look up business registration
5. Read recent (not old) reviews

### Step 2: Verify Supplier (1-2 hours)
1. Contact supplier with detailed questions
2. Check response time and professionalism
3. Verify Certificate of Analysis availability
4. Request sample images of products
5. Ask about discrete shipping

### Step 3: Place Order (30 minutes)
1. Write down what you're ordering (copy confirmation)
2. Note all important details (batch numbers, quantity)
3. Choose secure payment method
4. Get tracking number when available
5. Keep all communications

### Step 4: Receive & Inspect (30 minutes)
1. Check packaging integrity
2. Verify products match order
3. Inspect labels and serial numbers
4. Check for proper sterile appearance (injectables)
5. Contact supplier immediately if issues

### Step 5: Verify Authenticity (1-2 hours)
1. Cross-check labels against official images
2. Look up batch numbers on CoA
3. Contact lab if CoA provided
4. Check for hologram authenticity
5. Consult community if unsure

## CERTIFICATE OF ANALYSIS (CoA) VERIFICATION

### What a Real CoA Has:
- ✅ Lab name and accreditation number
- ✅ Test dates (within reason)
- ✅ Batch/lot numbers matching product
- ✅ Specific tests performed (HPLC, etc.)
- ✅ Results with pass/fail status
- ✅ Lab contact information
- ✅ Professional formatting

### How to Verify CoA:
1. Note lab name from certificate
2. Find lab independently (Google, don't use supplier link)
3. Visit lab website directly
4. Look for accreditation status (ILAC, A2LA)
5. Call lab and ask: "Did you test batch [number]?"
6. Lab confirms or denies — they have records

### CoA Red Flags:
- ❌ Unknown lab not in directory
- ❌ Lab won't confirm they issued it
- ❌ No accreditation number listed
- ❌ Test dates are months old
- ❌ Batch numbers don't match product

## STORAGE & LONGEVITY

### Proper Storage:
- **Temperature:** Cool, dry place (60-75°F ideal)
- **Humidity:** Below 60% RH
- **Light:** Away from direct sunlight
- **Container:** Original packaging preferred
- **Location:** Not bathroom, not garage

### Shelf Life:
- **Injectables:** 2-4 years (if stored properly)
- **Oral tablets:** 1-3 years (vary by compound)
- **Avoid:** Freezing, extreme heat, moisture

### Signs of Degradation:
- ❌ Discoloration
- ❌ Cloudiness (injectables)
- ❌ Unusual smell
- ❌ Separation of layers
- ❌ Crystallization

## LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS BY COUNTRY

### United States
- Schedule III controlled substance
- Possession without prescription = felony
- Maximum penalties: 1 year prison, $1,000 fine
- Trafficking: 5-40 years prison
- Import carries additional penalties

### United Kingdom
- Schedule IV controlled substance
- Possession for personal use: enforcement discretionary
- Supply: Illegal, up to 14 years prison
- Import/export: Serious offense

### Canada
- Schedule F (Prescription only)
- Unauthorized possession: Felony
- Supply: Criminal offense
- Trafficking: Serious charges

### Australia
- Schedule III controlled substance
- Possession: Criminal offense
- Supply: Serious charges
- Import: Federal offense

**Research your jurisdiction before purchasing.**

## WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOUR ORDER ARRIVES

### Immediate Actions (First 30 minutes)
1. Don't open anything
2. Inspect outer packaging for damage
3. Take photos of packaging (evidence if needed)
4. Open carefully and document contents
5. Check that items match your order

### Verification Steps
1. Compare labels to official images
2. Check batch/lot numbers
3. Verify expiration dates
4. Inspect seal integrity
5. Smell test (injectables should be odorless)

### If Something is Wrong
- **Wrong product:** Contact supplier, request replacement
- **Counterfeit suspected:** Do not use, ask for replacement
- **Damaged packaging:** Document with photos, request replacement
- **Missing items:** Provide proof (photos, order confirmation)

### Safe Storage
- Transfer to food-grade containers (optional but safe)
- Label clearly
- Keep away from children/pets
- Store in cool, dark place
- Keep receipts/invoices separate

## COMMON SCAMS & HOW TO AVOID THEM

### Scam 1: "Upgrade Your Order"
- Supplier offers upgrade to premium product
- Requests additional payment
- Never sends the upgraded product
- **Prevention:** Don't engage with upgrade offers

### Scam 2: "Payment Failed"
- Claims payment didn't go through
- Requests payment again
- Already received first payment
- **Prevention:** Keep all transaction records

### Scam 3: "Customs Hold"
- Claims package seized by customs
- Offers to reship for additional fee
- Item doesn't exist
- **Prevention:** Use discrete shipping providers

### Scam 4: "Selective Sourcing"
- Offers rare compounds at low prices
- Takes payment
- Never ships
- **Prevention:** If price seems too good, it is

### Scam 5: "Trust Me"
- New supplier with no reviews
- Claims to be legitimate
- Disappears after payment
- **Prevention:** Only buy from established suppliers
